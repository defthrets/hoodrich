🧹 0.7.0 — 8 September 2026 The grow room and the pill press are gone. Both doors, both markers, both blips. They were freezing the game for people going in and out, and four rounds of work never proved the rooms were even there. The freeze itself is fixed at the root as well — a wait that could spin forever if the game paused mid-fade — so any door you add through the ini can't hang either. The mod stops deleting the world when one thing goes wrong. A single caught error used to tear down every scene, car and person the mod had placed and rebuild them the next frame — the "everything blinked out" moment. Now it just carries on. Every deprecated ScriptHookVDotNet call is replaced too, so it runs on newer SHVDN builds, and every native it uses was checked against SHVDN's own table. Stretch's counter. Extended mags actually stay on now — the locker was quietly putting the stock clip back over them after a death, which is why guns came back with a suppressor and no mag. X on a pad opens the parts, as the bar said it would. And the parts have their own pictures. Your people and the police. The Families never start a fight with an officer, but they finish one the officer starts. The block feels lived in. Guards put the gun down for a smoke or a drink now and then, never in step with each other. Everyone at Lamar's uses the furniture.
