0.6.1 — 7 September 2026 Two fixes off the first day of reports.
  • The phone stopped working at the safe house, and so did the kitchen. The game's own house scripts raise the mission flag and do not always clear it, and this mod stands down while that flag is up — right on a story mission, wrong in your own kitchen. The flag no longer stands it down inside the house. Every other reason still does. On 0.6.0, PauseDuringMission=false in the ini works around it.
  • The feed can be turned off on screen. The old setting only chose which side of the screen a post landed on; this one means neither, and nothing chimes either. The timeline still fills, so it is all there to read in the phone. FeedOnScreen under [Socials], a tick on the settings screen, or NumPad * to toggle it where you stand — which writes the answer back to the ini. FeedKey=None takes the key away.
0.6.0 — 6 September 2026 Three hundred and fifty-eight changes since 0.5.1, and more of them than usual are things the mod could not do at all before. THE STREET TAKEOVER
  • A junction taken over one night in three, some time between nine and four. Cars and people arrive from the surrounding streets, a ring of them parks facing in, and the rest burn out and spin in the middle until the blue lights arrive. It happens whether you go or not.
  • Two junctions now, on Carson and on Davis, and they take turns — one takeover at each in turn rather than a coin that can land the same way three times running.
  • Every mark was walked one at a time: the kerbs the cars park on, the pavement corners the people stand on, and the places in the middle the cars work. Nothing is placed or teleported — every car drives itself in.
  • Three cars turn up to every one: an Emperor Vectre in the set's green, an FR36 and a Gauntlet Hellfire. Everything else in the field is random.
  • Every car that competes is on competition suspension, asked for by the shop's own name for the part, and none of them wears a livery.
  • The burnouts do not pause. A performer keeps going for as long as there is empty road under it, and slows, stops and drives back when it has reached the crowd.
  • Spectator cars park on a walked kerb or they go home. No more cars settling in a traffic lane for the night.
  • The crowd cheers, films it on their phones, drinks, and gets out of the way of a car coming at them. Some of the set turn up on dirt bikes and stay on them, filming or burning out.
  • Flares in the middle. A stranger who drives into the circle gets pulled out and his car gets stamped on for a quarter of a minute.
  • The police come into the junction to break it up rather than stopping at the end of the street.
  • Post up at one and the crowd buys from you.
  • A button per junction on the settings screen starts one without waiting for the clock.
THE BLOCK, WHEN NOTHING IS HAPPENING
  • Groups of the set walk the back streets with a beer or a cigarette, stop now and again for a drink or a drag, and talk to each other — one says something and another answers, loud enough to hear from the end of the road.
  • Lowriders one night and donks the next: three in a line, green, hydraulics working, music up, full of the set.
  • Corners with people on them — leaning on a wall, on a beer, on a phone, smoking, a boombox playing, somebody tagging the wall on the game's own spray loop with black paint coming out of the can. The lot behind Innocence, the Checkout, the B/Holes lot and the shops in Strawberry.
  • Each corner rolls its own day, six in ten, so some days one is empty and another is not.
  • A bike meet at Leroy's two nights in five, and a couple sat talking at the Strawberry shops.
  • Every hangout posts about itself on the feed once a night when its people come out.
  • Three of the set on each of nine walked corners, and the Families female joins the patrols, the parties and the takeover.
  • Everyone of the set carries. The ones stood about without a gun in their hands have one holstered, drawn when something starts, and none of them runs from a fight in front of them.
  • The leaders carry a pistol and are not killed by it going wrong.
  • The Families never turn on Franklin. They cannot cross into beef with him, their leaders take a knock from him without drawing, and naming them on the feed does not send a car round.
  • No unprovoked raid in the first ten minutes after the mod loads.
