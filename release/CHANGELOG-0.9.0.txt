0.9.0 -- 14 September 2026


VERNON HAS A JOB NOW, AND IT IS THE ONE HE HAS BEEN TALKING ABOUT. The man on
the wall outside Leroy's Electrical finally asks you for something. He walks
you down into the basement he keeps calling square footage, shows you round an
operation whose vault is a filing cabinet and whose extraction is a fan pointed
up the stairs, and then puts it to you: a kilo off an Armenian at Rogers Scrap
for twenty-two five, eight of it already paid, and he is coming with you
because it is his money and he has never met the man. It goes how you expect
and he does not. He carries the money out in a paper bag, puts it down to
shoot, and goes back for it the moment the shooting stops.

HE ALSO HAS A CAR. A black Dorado parked down the side of the shop, chrome
rims, green tubes, smoked glass, OGVEE on a Sprunk plate, sat down on its back
axle -- and locked, every hour of the save except the one he needs you for.
The suspension is this mod's own rather than borrowed from a mod you might not
have installed.

A BAG YOU CAN LOSE. Twenty slots, worn on your back, dropped with a key and
picked up off the pavement, and it is the only way to move weight. It keeps
what is in it across a restart and across wherever you left it. Die wearing it
and it comes off where you fell with everything still inside, which makes
losing it something you decide by not walking back for it. Die without it and
your pockets pay, the way they always did.

METH IS SPEED AND NOTHING THAT SLOWS YOU DOWN. The number said sprint and the
animation said last orders: forty per cent quicker on his feet, under a drunk
stagger and a world running at seven eighths speed. What you felt was a man
wading. A hurried walk, no slow motion, no shake. The hallucinations stay where
they were -- they are the screen effects and those already wait for the second
dose, which is what a higher dose is for.

HEROIN NO LONGER PUTS YOU ON THE FLOOR. It chained into the nod: on your side
for the minutes after, which is honest about the drug and a long time to spend
looking at the back of your own head with no way to get up. It is the one
effect in the mod that took the game off you rather than changing it.

DRUGS LAST TWICE AS LONG. Every high is doubled -- weed a hundred seconds to
two hundred, cocaine five minutes to ten, heroin seven to fourteen. The
comedown after them is untouched, because the wreckage is the price rather
than the effect.

THE FAM APP GETS THREE PAGES: your set, the map of who holds what, and who you
have got beef with.

EVERY VOICE AT THE SAME LEVEL. The pack was recorded over months and sounded
like it -- fifteen decibels between the loudest man and the quietest. Every
take is measured and levelled now, and the volume slider actually moves them,
which it had not been doing at all.

AND A LOT OF THINGS THAT WERE QUIETLY WRONG:

  Ten rows on the settings screen wrote keys nothing reads, so moving them
  changed nothing after a reload. All ten repointed.

  Going down Vernon's stairs swapped Los Santos for the online map, for a room
  that was in the story map all along.

  D-pad right moved the inventory cursor AND put the thing under it in your
  bag, which read as the mod eating your shopping.

  The kit on a dead cat said Keon Peralta, 5'5", Hispanic, ninety eight
  dollars. People only now.

  Mission briefs from anybody but Lamar were filed under his name and never
  played. Every one of Vernon's was silent.

  The set's cars at a meet go back to West Coast Classics.

  Bodies you drag are held by the wrist and ragdoll properly rather than
  jolting into the air -- and you can start the drag off the loot card.
