📍 POSTED UP

A drug-dealing and gang mod for GTA V, built from scratch. You pick the corner,
not the customer.

You run with the Chamberlain Gangster Families. Get hold of product, take it home
and cut it, stand somewhere busy and let the trade come to you — and try to be
gone before the police or somebody else's people decide you have been there long
enough.

Everything runs off a phone that replaces the in-game one. Your weapon wheel is
left exactly as it was.

🔁 THE LOOP

1. 📦 GET HOLD OF IT. Gerald fronts you the first two packages for nothing, so you
   do not need money to start. After that you buy — off him by the gram, or off
   the man at the port by the brick, once you have proved you come back.

2. 🧪 CUT IT. Weight bought by the brick will not sell to anybody. It goes to the
   sink in Denise's kitchen first, and how hard you step on it is the only real
   decision in the mod. 100g at half strength is 200g to sell. It is also
   obviously weak, and the block can tell.

3. 📍 STAND SOMEWHERE. You do not pick customers, you pick a SPOT. A busy pavement
   sells fast and gets you noticed fast; an empty street does neither. Buyers walk
   up and ask for an amount, and what one block pays is not what the next one pays.

4. 🚨 DO NOT STILL BE THERE. Heat builds where you stand and sticks to the GROUND,
   not to you. A different block is a clean start, and the one you burned is still
   burned when you come back.

📋 WHAT'S IN IT

🏴 NINE GANGS — Families, Ballas, Vagos, Aztecas, Marabunta Grande, Lost MC,
   Triads, Armenian Mob, Kkangpae. Named leaders standing on their own turf, their
   own colours, their own standing with you. Rep, bodies, money and beef are
   tracked per gang, not as one number.

🤝 THIRTEEN SUPPLIERS TO FIND — eight gang corners, three independents, plus
   Gerald and Tao at the port. A dealer is a man on a corner until you have stood
   in front of him.

📈 A REPUTATION SEPARATE FROM YOUR RANK — sell decent product and your name
   climbs. Step on everything and it does not, and a refusal is never just one
   lost sale. They remember, they tell people, and some of them want to do
   something about it.

🎯 SIX JOBS FROM LAMAR — a straightener at the Chamberlain courts, a drive-by
   through Jamestown, going over a rival's tags, a cut house in Rancho, getting
   the Ballas off Grove, and a route through La Mesa. He rides out with you, keeps
   up, and does not shut up.

🚔 A POLICE SYSTEM THAT IS NOT A WANTED LEVEL — patrols work the blocks whether or
   not anybody is after you. Work one spot long enough and they stop driving past
   and start stopping. Searched holding product costs you the product; holding
   nothing costs you a fine, which is why dropping a bag before they reach you
   works.

📉 BLOCK SATURATION — every gram sold makes the next customer there slower to
   show, and it recovers while you are elsewhere.

🏠 STASH HOUSE RAIDS — hold too much and somebody comes for it while you are out.

📱 A SOCIAL FEED OF 174 PEOPLE with fixed handles and avatars, and 1,664 template
   lines across 106 event sets, filled in with the block you are on, the set you
   run with and the money involved. Post up and the word goes out in a language
   that does not say anything outright.

🏁 A STREET TAKEOVER, one night in three, at one of two junctions that take turns
   — cars burning out and spinning in the middle, a crowd stood on the pavement
   corners, and nothing placed anywhere: every car drives itself in. It happens
   whether you go or not, and if you post up at it the crowd buys from you.

🍺 A BLOCK THAT IS LIVED ON WHEN NOTHING IS HAPPENING — crews walking the back
   streets with a beer or a cigarette, stopping for a drink and a drag, talking
   loud enough that you hear them before you see them. Lowriders one night and
   donks the next. Corners with people on them, a bike meet at Leroy's, and 🐕 a
   quarter of the crews walking a dog on a real rope lead.

👕 SIX OUTFITS AT THE CLOSET, hung up and named the way the game's own wardrobe
   does it, plus rows for how he walks and how he holds a gun.

🚗 SERVE OUT OF THE CAR WINDOW — post up in the driver's seat of a stopped car and
   they come to the door instead of the pavement.

💊 SEVEN PRODUCTS, ELEVEN CARS FROM HAO, GUNS FROM STRETCH — plus bodyguards, turf
   war, dead drops, a stash house, a low-end mod shop at under half the price of
   the one over the river, driverless cars off the phone, and a spray can with
   thirteen colours.

😵 YOU CAN TAKE YOUR OWN PRODUCT, and each of the seven does something different —
   weed slows the world, coke makes you fast and bright, meth is a high you can
   feel, and heroin is the one that ends with you on the ground. The second one is
   the one you notice; four in you and you wake up somewhere else.

🎤 152 RECORDED LINES — Gerald, Lamar, Tao Cheng, Hao and Stretch say what the
   screen says, and the ones nobody has recorded are read rather than heard.

🔧 BUILT THE AWKWARD WAY, ON PURPOSE

🚫 ZERO EXTERNAL DEPENDENCIES. No LemonUI, no NativeUI, no Newtonsoft, no Lua, no
   OpenIV, no asset replacement, no installer. One DLL and some JSON. Nothing in
   your scripts folder can lose a version fight with it, and no game file is
   modified.

📝 EVERYTHING IS DATA. 11 JSON files you can edit — drugs, prices, gangs, turf,
   dealers, missions, cars, weapons, scenery and every line of the social feed.
   Break one and the mod says so in the log and falls back to its built-in copy
   rather than failing to load. 102 settings in a commented INI; delete any line
   to go back to its default.

🎨 107 ICONS, DRAWN FROM SCRATCH for this mod. No Rockstar assets ship with it.

122,000 lines of C# across 173 files · 11 data files · 107 original icons
Works on GTA V Legacy and Enhanced from the same DLL. Single player only — do not
take this near GTA Online.

🛠️ INSTALL

1. ⚠️ UNBLOCK THE ZIP BEFORE EXTRACTING. Right-click it → Properties → tick
   Unblock → OK. Windows marks downloaded files and ScriptHookVDotNet silently
   refuses to load a marked DLL — the mod just is not there, with nothing in any
   log to explain it. This is the single most common reason it "doesn't work".

2. 📁 DRAG THE SCRIPTS FOLDER INTO YOUR GTA V FOLDER — the one with GTA5.exe in
   it, normally C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto V.
   Say yes to merging.

You should end up with scripts\Hoodrich.dll, scripts\Hoodrich.ini and the
scripts\Hoodrich\ folder beside them. That is the whole install — no game files
are modified.

🔄 UPGRADING? Drop it over the top, old saves load — but copy scripts\Hoodrich\ as
well as the DLL.

▶️ GETTING STARTED

🗺️ Gerald is the only marker on your map. Go see him at the flats on Grove — he
puts the first lot of product in your hand for free. Then open the phone, go to
Dealing, and post up somewhere with people on it.

💡 The one thing nobody works out on their own: WEIGHT YOU BUY WILL NOT SELL UNTIL
IT HAS BEEN CUT AND BAGGED at the sink in Denise's kitchen. Anything you are given
is already bagged.

🎮 CONTROLS: the phone button opens everything. Arrows move, Enter chooses,
Backspace goes back. Right on the d-pad talks to people. Every key is rebindable
in Hoodrich.ini.

🗑️ UNINSTALL: delete those three things. Nothing else to undo.

📜 CHANGELOG


🚗 0.6.2 — 7 September 2026

YOU CAN SERVE OUT OF THE CAR WINDOW. Posting up used to have one flat refusal in
it: get out of the car first. Now you can do it sitting in the driver's seat of a
stopped car, and the window winds down as you start. Buyers walk to the driver's
door rather than to the bonnet — the mark is worked out from the car's own width,
so they stand at the glass of a van and at the glass of a coupe rather than a
metre off one of them. He keeps his seat through the handoff, using the game's own
traffic-stop gesture: the one where a driver passes his licence out to an officer
at the door. Same movement, different thing in the hand. Driving off packs it up,
measured in metres rather than in speed, so shuffling forward to let somebody past
is staying put.

SIX OUTFITS AT THE CLOSET, AND HOW HE CARRIES HIMSELF. The wardrobe at Denise's
could dress him and remember what he had on, and that was all — one look, and
changing it lost the last one. There are six pegs on it now: hang up what you are
wearing, name it or let it call itself Outfit 3, and put it back on whenever you
are at the rail. Two more rows next to the body choose how he walks and how he
holds a gun, including the game's own gangster walk and its gang shooting style,
and the change lands as you scroll it.

A DOG ON A LEAD. A quarter of the crews walking the back streets bring one with
them — a pug, a rottweiler or a shepherd — and the lead is one of the game's own
ropes, pinned between the dog's neck and the man's hand so it hangs and swings and
goes taut on its own.

FIVE MORE OF THE SET'S CARS, AND THEY ARE BLACK OR GREEN AND NOTHING ELSE. A
Greenwood, an Impaler LX, a Manana Custom, a Tahoma Coupe and a Vamos. Every car
the set drove used to be the same green, which reads as a fleet rather than a set;
half are metallic black now, all of them on dark smoke glass, dropped springs and
a silver pearl, with no other mods on any of them.

NOBODY OF OURS SHOOTS AT THE POLICE. Everyone of the set carries, so one patrol
rolling past a hangout was the whole night gone. Anybody wearing one of the mod's
gang groups who picks a fight with an officer is taken out of it and leaves
instead, which is what people on a corner actually do. The two homies riding with
you are the exception and only half of it: they stop shooting and they do not run,
because running would leave you in the car.

THREE THINGS AN AUDIT FOUND THAT HAD NEVER WORKED. The crews on foot could not be
told to stand down — the switch was there, its comment said the house script wired
it, and the house script never did, so they kept turning up through raids and
takeovers. Lamar's tag job never wrote a tag: the glyph table, the layout and the
word-picker were all correct and none of them was ever called, so every tag in the
game has been the random splatter that is meant to be the fallback. And two gangs
were drawn the wrong colour on the map, both cyan gangs sitting on the blue blip.

SMALLER THINGS. Gerald is off the ecstasy and stops advertising an uncut lot
nobody can buy. Nothing at a takeover fits a wheelie bar any more, and a boot held
open has speakers in it. The Asbo on the pad at Lamar's, on competition springs.
The car by the decks is a Buccaneer Custom. Franklin's driving is back to vanilla
— the window only comes down when he is serving.


🩹 0.6.1 — 7 September 2026

Two fixes off the first day of reports.

THE PHONE STOPPED WORKING ONCE YOU GOT TO THE SAFE HOUSE, and so did the kitchen:
you could not call Tao Cheng and the counter would not answer. Walking into
Franklin's runs the game's own house scripts — the bed, the wardrobe, the
television — and any of them can raise the mission flag and then leave it raised
while you potter about indoors. This mod stands down while that flag is up, which
is the right thing to do on a story mission and the wrong thing to do in your own
kitchen. From where you are standing it looks like the mod simply died, with
nothing on screen to say why. The flag no longer stands it down inside the house.
Every other reason still does — the controls being taken off you, a cutscene, a
faded screen, the pause menu. If you are staying on 0.6.0, PauseDuringMission=false
in the ini works around it.

THE SOCIAL FEED CAN BE TURNED OFF ON SCREEN, which it could not be before. The old
setting only ever chose which side of the screen a post landed on; this one means
neither. Nothing is drawn, nothing goes into the game's own notification stack, and
so nothing chimes at you either. The timeline still fills in the background, so
every post is there to read in the phone whenever you want it — it just stops
narrating itself while you play.

There are three ways to work it: FeedOnScreen under [Socials] in Hoodrich.ini, a
tick on the mod's own settings screen, and a key — NumPad * by default — that
toggles it where you stand and writes the answer back to the ini, so it survives a
restart. Set FeedKey=None if you would rather have the key back.


🏁 0.6.0 — 6 September 2026

Three hundred and fifty-eight changes since 0.5.1, and more of them than usual are
things the mod could not do at all before.

🏎️ THE STREET TAKEOVER

- A junction taken over one night in three, some time between nine and four. Cars
  and people arrive from the surrounding streets, a ring of them parks facing in,
  and the rest burn out and spin in the middle until the blue lights arrive. It
  happens whether you go or not.
- TWO JUNCTIONS NOW, on Carson and on Davis, and they take turns — one takeover at
  each in turn rather than a coin that can land the same way three times running.
- Every mark was walked one at a time: the kerbs the cars park on, the pavement
  corners the people stand on, and the places in the middle the cars work. Nothing
  is placed or teleported — every car drives itself in.
- THREE CARS TURN UP TO EVERY ONE: an Emperor Vectre in the set's green, an FR36
  and a Gauntlet Hellfire. Everything else in the field is random.
- Every car that competes is on competition suspension, asked for by the shop's
  own name for the part, and none of them wears a livery.
- The burnouts do not pause. A performer keeps going for as long as there is empty
  road under it, and slows, stops and drives back when it has reached the crowd.
- Spectator cars park on a walked kerb or they go home. No more cars settling in a
  traffic lane for the night.
- The crowd cheers, films it on their phones, drinks, and gets out of the way of a
  car coming at them. Some of the set turn up on dirt bikes and stay on them,
  filming or burning out.
- Flares in the middle. A stranger who drives into the circle gets pulled out and
  his car gets stamped on for a quarter of a minute.
- The police come into the junction to break it up rather than stopping at the end
  of the street.
- POST UP AT ONE AND THE CROWD BUYS FROM YOU.
- A button per junction on the settings screen starts one without waiting for the
  clock.

🍻 THE BLOCK, WHEN NOTHING IS HAPPENING

- Groups of the set walk the back streets with a beer or a cigarette, stop now and
  again for a drink or a drag, and talk to each other — one says something and
  another answers, loud enough to hear from the end of the road.
- Lowriders one night and donks the next: three in a line, green, hydraulics
  working, music up, full of the set.
- Corners with people on them — leaning on a wall, on a beer, on a phone, smoking,
  a boombox playing, somebody tagging the wall on the game's own spray loop with
  black paint coming out of the can. The lot behind Innocence, the Checkout, the
  B/Holes lot and the shops in Strawberry.
- Each corner rolls its own day, six in ten, so some days one is empty and another
  is not.
- A bike meet at Leroy's two nights in five, and a couple sat talking at the
  Strawberry shops.
- Every hangout posts about itself on the feed once a night when its people come
  out.
- Three of the set on each of nine walked corners, and the Families female joins
  the patrols, the parties and the takeover.
- Everyone of the set carries. The ones stood about without a gun in their hands
  have one holstered, drawn when something starts, and none of them runs from a
  fight in front of them.
- The leaders carry a pistol and are not killed by it going wrong.
- THE FAMILIES NEVER TURN ON FRANKLIN. They cannot cross into beef with him, their
  leaders take a knock from him without drawing, and naming them on the feed does
  not send a car round.
- No unprovoked raid in the first ten minutes after the mod loads.
