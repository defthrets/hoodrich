📍 POSTED UP

A drug-dealing and gang mod for GTA V. You pick the corner, not the customer.

You run with the Chamberlain Gangster Families. Buy product, cut it at home, stand
somewhere busy and let the trade come to you — and be gone before the police or
somebody else's people decide you have been there long enough.

It all runs off a phone that replaces the in-game one. Your weapon wheel is left
alone.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔁 THE LOOP

1. 📦 GET IT. Gerald fronts you the first two packages free. After that it is by
   the gram off him, or by the brick at the port.

2. 🧪 CUT IT. Brick weight will not sell until it is cut at the sink in Denise's
   kitchen. How hard you step on it is the only real decision in the mod — 100g at
   half strength is 200g to sell, and the block can tell.

3. 📍 POST UP. You pick a spot, not a customer. A busy pavement sells fast and gets
   you noticed fast. What one block pays is not what the next one pays.

4. 🚨 MOVE ON. Heat sticks to the ground, not to you. A new block is a clean start;
   the one you burned is still burned when you come back.

📋 WHAT'S IN IT

🏴 Nine gangs — named leaders, own turf, own colours, own standing with you.
🤝 Thirteen suppliers to track down.
🎯 Six jobs from Lamar — he rides out with you and does not shut up.
🚔 Police that are not a wanted level — patrols work your blocks either way, and
   being searched holding product costs you the product.
🏁 Street takeovers one night in three — burnouts, a crowd, and they buy from you
   if you post up.
🍺 A block that lives without you — crews out drinking and smoking, lowriders and
   donks cruising, corners with people on them, dogs on leads.
📱 A social feed of 174 people that reacts to what you actually do.
📉 Block saturation — worked corners go quiet, and recover while you are away.
🏠 Stash house raids — hold too much and somebody comes for it.
💊 Seven products, and you can take them yourself.
🚗 Eleven cars from Hao, a cheap mod shop, and driverless rides off the phone.
🔫 Guns from Stretch, plus bodyguards, turf war, dead drops and a spray can with
   thirteen colours.
👕 Six outfits at the closet, plus how he walks and how he holds a gun.
🎤 152 recorded lines — Gerald, Lamar, Tao, Hao and Stretch speak.

🔧 NO DEPENDENCIES, NO GAME FILES TOUCHED

One DLL and some JSON. No LemonUI, no NativeUI, no OpenIV, no asset replacement,
no installer. It will not fight your graphics mods, car packs or map edits, and
uninstalling is deleting three things.

Everything is editable if you want it to be: 11 JSON files and 102 settings in a
commented INI.

Legacy and Enhanced from the same DLL. Single player only — do not take this near
GTA Online.

🛠️ INSTALL

1. ⚠️ UNBLOCK THE ZIP BEFORE YOU EXTRACT IT. Right-click → Properties → tick
   Unblock → OK. Windows marks downloaded files and ScriptHookVDotNet will not
   load a marked DLL — the mod simply is not there, with nothing to tell you why.
   This is the number one reason it "doesn't work".

2. 📁 Drag the scripts folder into your GTA V folder, the one with GTA5.exe in it.
   Say yes to merging.

You should end up with scripts\Hoodrich.dll, scripts\Hoodrich.ini and a
scripts\Hoodrich\ folder.

🔄 UPGRADING: drop it over the top — old saves load. Copy the scripts\Hoodrich\
   folder as well as the DLL.
🗑️ UNINSTALLING: delete those three things. Nothing else to undo.

▶️ GETTING STARTED

🗺️ Gerald is the only marker on your map. See him at the flats on Grove and he puts
the first lot in your hand free. Then open the phone, go to Dealing, and post up
somewhere with people on it.

💡 The one thing nobody works out on their own: WEIGHT YOU BUY WILL NOT SELL UNTIL
IT IS CUT AND BAGGED at the sink in Denise's kitchen. Anything you are given is
already bagged.

🎮 The phone button opens everything. Arrows move, Enter chooses, Backspace goes
back, right on the d-pad talks to people. Every key is rebindable in Hoodrich.ini.
