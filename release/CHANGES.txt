  POSTED UP  --  CHANGES

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.3.0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Everything below came out of playing the thing. Most of it is fixes.


  THE PHONE

  * Messages. A new app, second on the phone, holding every text anybody has
    ever sent you. They used to go to the game's feed and be gone in seconds, so
    a plug naming the street he was parked on was information you had one chance
    to catch.
  * You can text from it. Every plug is listed whether he has written to you or
    not, and so are the homies -- send from the thread and they come, same as
    the contacts page. Your own messages appear in the conversation.
  * Three dots while he is writing back, and his answer arrives without you
    having to close the phone.
  * Messages sits second and Dealing fourth. Contacts has not moved.
  * The settings screen lost the six sliders nobody needed, and two ways it
    could trap you in it.


  THE BLOCK

  * The party is an evening now. Seven at night until two in the morning. In
    daylight it is a couple of people and the man watching the back fence, which
    is what a yard belonging to somebody looks like at two in the afternoon.
  * The people who only come for it ARRIVE -- they park up on the road and walk
    in through the gate, and each one starts what he came to do when he reaches
    his own spot.
  * The music keeps the party's hours instead of playing around the clock.
  * A couple of them wander rather than standing on one spot all night.
  * Somebody's dog is out in the yard.
  * The Families never start on Franklin on his own block, and have stopped
    telling him he is in the wrong neighbourhood.
  * The yard talks again. A fix for the above had quietly silenced the party as
    well, the two daytime regulars included.
  * Nobody in this mod wears a crash helmet.


  WORK

  * Drive-bys. The homies stay in the car. They used to climb out mid-spin to
    fight on foot, which made the job unusable -- the cause was an order the mod
    itself was giving them, to get into a car they were already sitting in.
  * "Get 'em off Grove" is a drive-by now rather than a job you park for, with
    the dialogue rewritten to match.
  * Dying on the port run ends the port run, rather than sending you back to a
    burnt-out truck. The same goes for every job that works that way.
  * Gerald's truck has BIG G on the plate, lives on its kerb whether or not you
    need it, and stays locked when you do not.
  * The tag run could get into a state where it could be neither finished nor
    cancelled.
  * Your graffiti now survives closing the game. It is in the save.
  * Spraying lasts three seconds longer, and the can sits in his hand properly.
  * The block comes out at you once per tag run rather than at every wall, and
    they come with baseball bats or their hands instead of pistols.
  * Burning the car is the job, so it stops handing out a wanted level for it.
  * The store robbery cannot be declined into a dead end, the clerk comes back
    if you kill him, and Lamar stops squaring up when you bump into him.
  * Lamar walks to his own bike, gets on it, and follows you off-road.
  * The drive home keeps its directions instead of losing them at the first
    shot.
  * Lamar stops offering a job you have finished and starts offering to run it
    past you again. He also stopped saying it twice in a row.


  THE TRADE

  * Powder is weighed and pills are counted. What Gerald brings for the bar
    trade is powder, so it now reads in grams -- it is what goes INTO a press,
    and nobody presses pills out of pills.
  * Deliveries stop announcing twenty grams as "0 kilos in the house".
  * Gerald and Tao walk out to their vehicles rather than running, and Tao
    actually staggers now.
  * Cutting no longer launders your bulk up to pure by accident.
  * The grow room and the pill press have people working in them.
  * Cars bought from Hao are written to the save the moment they are paid for,
    and stay where you left them across a restart.
  * Hao's Baller is the ST-D.
  * Cheng's men at the docks are Chinese, work out of step with each other, and
    their subtitle draws properly.


  THE REST

  * Every marker on the map has a name, including the one that was not ours.
  * Panels arrive and leave instead of doing neither, and stop cutting text at
    the corners.
  * A paragraph break could draw on top of the paragraph above it.
  * Aunt Denise stops shouting through the door -- properly this time. She used
    to get half a word out before being cut off, and she started again whenever
    a menu was open.
  * The grow room and pill press put you back where you came in, rather than in
    the desert.
  * Nobody smokes a xanax bar.
  * An audit of the whole mod, which is where a good number of the above came
    from.
