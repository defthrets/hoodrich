0.8.0 - 11 September 2026 A language setting, and nine languages to go in it. [General] Language, the first row of the settings screen, English by default: English (UK and US), Portugues (Brasil), Espanol, Francais, Deutsch, Russkiy, Polski, Chinese (Simplified) and Hindi in Latin letters, the way it is typed -- the game's fonts have no Devanagari. Each one is a data file, scripts\Hoodrich\lang\<code>.json, English on the left and the language on the right, so it survives every update and needs no fork; anything a file does not cover shows in English. Looked up as it is drawn, so the screen changes as you pick. Chinese needs the game itself in Chinese, or its characters are not in the font. The dialogue, the feed and the log stay English by design. The phone stops flickering, and the log says why if it ever does again. Every new face on the feed, and the handset prop on every take-out, was made by blocking the tick on a model load -- and a blocked tick draws nothing, so the phone went dark for as long as each model took to stream. Both stream across ticks now and the phone stays on. Every system the tick runs is a named segment, and one that hands the frame back is logged by name with how many frames the HUD was dark for. One list of rectangles, counted for the whole machine. The game keeps one list of rectangles a frame for every script on it and drops the rest past about three hundred and fifty, so the mod that draws last pays for everybody's frame. The set now counts into one shared tally -- Posted Up, Bare Minimum, Fumes, Five0 Patrol, Bloody Mess -- and every log says what the busiest frame cost and who spent it. Decoration asks the tally first: past the soft line, whoever is over their share loses their sparkle until the room is measured to be there again, and the instruments never go. The colours come out as picked. Blood as the one paint texture could not do it -- the colour multiplies the texture, blood is red, green came out bronze and cyan grey. Paint is the texture for anything with a colour in it again; black keeps blood, whose shape is the best on the rack. CanDecal for the colours, CanDecalDark for black, in the ini. A skips the phone's boot screen. The seal turning on the handset's first open is a piece of character once and a toll booth after that. A on a pad or Enter on a keyboard cuts straight to the apps, and the boot stays skipped for the session.
