## 0.9.7

**loads on any scripthookvdotnet 3 now.** 3.6, the nightlies, whatever you've got. you
don't need 3.9.0.0 any more — that one was the cause of half the "doesn't work" reports.

**the blip that drove off on random cars** — his buffalo's and his bike's mark was
attached to the vehicle and the game reuses the handle. one blip on the map now,
attached to nothing.

**cars vanishing off hao's lot after you bought them** — the lot was deleting a car it
couldn't prove was its own. it can now.

**gang war crashes** — a war won't start on a full block any more, it uses two car
models instead of nine, and the limits are in the ini ([Block] PedsBusy / CarsBusy).
lower them if a busy block is crashing you.

**the bag** — lands on the ground now however you died, and there's "bring my bag here"
in settings for one that's already lost.

**your keys back** — PhoneButton=false leaves the up arrow to the game, and
FranklinsCar= / FranklinsBike= left blank leave his own car and bike alone.
