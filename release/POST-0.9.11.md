## 0.9.11

**five more corners** — one per set, all in person only, cheap and cut to three quarters (buy big
and it comes clean). pelon for the vagos in cypress flats, nelson for marabunta up in el burro
heights, jae for the kkangpae in little seoul, vahe for the armenians by the freeway in la puerta,
og reese for the ballas in davis. cold the first time like flaco, first buy squares you with that
set on their blocks. three of theirs stand round each of them.

**every corner man is on the map from the start** — dimmed in his set's colour, brighter once
you've met him. kkangpae are dark blue now.

**lamar's yard** — the hanging weed's gone, there's a second wall, a skate ramp, containers and a
couch along the east side.

**texture loss** — the mod was keeping every model it ever asked for loaded for the whole session
(130-odd cars and faces from one takeover). it lets go of anything unused for 20 seconds now,
takeovers bring fewer kinds of car and fewer faces, and the big scenes load nearer.

**stretch's guns** — two guns of the same calibre were coming back from a load with double the
rounds. fixed. rounds you buy for a gun you didn't buy off him are kept too, and attachments
fitted right before a save stick.

**npc mind** — its panel no longer opens on top of the dealers, the leaders, hao, vernon or
stretch. a pill sold through its talk is a whole pill.

**translations** — everything that was missing since 0.9.9 in all eight languages, and the old
ones read through and fixed (latin american spanish throughout).

also: a door-only dealer stops giving you a number he won't answer, and the log now names every
missing voice line, not just some.

needs ScriptHookV and ScriptHookVDotNet 3.6 or newer. the -full zip has scripthookvdotnet in it.
