0.9.6 -- 18 September 2026


VERNON SELLS YOU POWDER NOW. He was meant to from the day he got his number:
finish his job, drive off, and he texts to say the basement is stocked -- and
then every order off him ended in "Vernon: nobody could make the run",
because his entry in dealers.json named no model for the man behind the
wheel. He drives his own Dorado to the house now, the one parked down the
side of the shop, rims and glass and green tubes and all, and walks it in
himself.


AND HE CANNOT BE LOST ANY MORE. There were three ways to end up with a Vernon
who would never pitch the job again -- stood there telling you he would walk
you down, in front of a door he would never open. Accept it while you were on
one of Lamar's, or before you were in a set, and the runner refused but the
"he has explained it" flag was already written. Quit the game with the job
running. Or press the settings row, which un-did the mission and nothing
else. All three read as "taken and not failed", which is what the offer used
to route on. It routes on the job being DONE now and nothing else; accepting
only counts once the runner has actually started it; and the settings row
un-counts the whole job, so he asks you again from the top.


TWO KILOS A CALL, AS WRITTEN. The door screen never read his order cap --
the rows come off the lot size -- so he was offering three, six and twelve
kilos. The rows are 500g, 1kg and 2kg.


AND THE SMALLER THINGS AROUND HIM. He starts the debrief himself when you
walk up with his kilo, instead of waiting for a button he was refusing to
show. He is not teleported onto his wall as the hand-in conversation opens
any more -- he walks back to it after, from wherever the talk left him. The
wall is empty while he is the one driving to your door, so there is one of
him in the street rather than two. And the record list tells the truth about
his lines: his own on the named files, none of the shared fallbacks he does
not say, and the four doorstep pools it had never listed.


IT SAYS WHEN YOUR SCRIPTHOOKVDOTNET IS TOO OLD. The commonest report on the
page is a MissingMethodException naming a native the loader does not have,
and the mod had no answer for it -- it signed the crash off as one for the
author. It reads the version it was built against out of its own manifest
now, compares it with the one running it, and says which is older and where
to get the newer one, on screen and in the log. A loader that is the same or
newer sees nothing.
