0.9.10 -- 1 October 2026


FLACO, ON DUTCH LONDON. A Vagos dealer at the barred door marked 1327 in
Rancho, selling coke, heroin and meth, in person only, with one of his set
beside him holding a compact rifle. The first time, he wants to know what
somebody from Chamberlain is doing at his door; keep it about the money and he
sells. That first buy squares you with the Vagos on their own blocks -- they
stop starting on you for walking them or serving on them -- but their wars are
still their wars, and a raid is still a raid.


DEACON, AT THE LOST CLUBHOUSE off Mirror Park. Meth, crack, coke and LSD, in
person only, with three of the club round him. Cold at first like Flaco, and
the first buy squares you with the Lost on their turf the same way. He has an
account on the feed. Both of them are found by going there: a blip when you are
close until you have met them, on the map for good after.


EIGHT STASH HOUSES TO RENT, on the game's own street names. Apartment 9A at
$400 a week, 77 at $600, 32 and 47 at $300, 13 at $550 and 90 at $500, in the
low-end apartment; 4 at $750 and 7 at $700 -- two ways in -- in the mid-range
one. Every stash house holds the one stock.


THE PLUG COMES TO ANY OF THEM. Ring one within twenty metres of a stash house
you rent and he sets off from round its corner, out of sight, pulls up outside
and walks the package to the front door. Ordering from Parkview works now: the
phone greyed every plug out there with "call him from the house", so the
delivery added in 0.9.9 could never be placed.


TWO NEW APPS. Dynasty 8 lists every place there is to rent, yours first with
the soonest rent at the top: pay up to twelve weeks ahead, put it on the map,
or give the lease up and get the weeks you paid ahead back -- and rent a new
one straight from the phone. Browser opens the game's own internet.


FRANKLIN TYPES HIS POSTS. When the mod posts for him -- posting up, a job done,
a meal ordered -- he takes the phone out and types it for a couple of seconds,
and can walk while he does. Anything that needs his hands stops him, and the
post goes up cut off where his thumb stopped. Posts you choose yourself still
go up the moment you pick them.


FROM THE MOD PAGE.

  The redneck attacks. The Lost were turning up in Chamberlain as hillbillies:
  a hillbilly was on their list of members, and raids, corner drive-bys and
  paybacks took whichever of the list was already loaded, which in South Los
  Santos was nearly always him. The Lost are bikers only now.

  The shooting that never stopped. The set's crews went for anybody from any
  rival set who walked past, every fifteen seconds, whatever you had done. Now
  only a set you are actually at war with.

  Crashing in shootouts while posted up. The feed's pictures are taken of real
  people, kept out of sight thirty metres over wherever you were standing --
  and in a gang fight they counted as targets nobody could hit, stacked right
  over your corner. They belong to nobody now. While a fight is on near your
  corner, it sends no customers, no police stop, no drive-by and no patrol, and
  the log writes every line until it is over, so if it still goes down the log
  says where.

  Franklin's car. Swapping his Buffalo S for the STX is on the settings screen
  again, and back in Hoodrich.ini under [Cars]. His own Buffalo keeps the
  game's car, and whatever car pack replaces it.

  Missing textures alongside a big graphics mod. The mod's scenery can be
  switched off on the settings screen, or with Enabled=false under [Scenery]:
  the places still work, emptier. The gambling den lets go of the casino's
  models when you leave it.

  Switched off from the phone with no way back. While Posted Up is off, the
  game tells you the key that turns it back on -- / on the number pad -- and
  the line in Hoodrich.ini.

  A line in Hoodrich.ini twice. The first one counts, which is the one the
  settings screen writes, and the log says so. That was Language=English not
  taking.

  Lamar's pistol kept coming back. A gun you get rid of yourself stays gone.
  Loading a save, dying or being arrested still hands back what you bought.

  The wardrobe camera no longer goes out through the wall in a small room.

  Takeover police cars no longer vanish when the officers get out, and the
  officers are not deleted mid-stride when it ends.


FIXES. The corner's drive-bys and payback crews fire in bursts out of the
window, the way the game's own do. A stop and search on your corner takes an
officer on foot, not one driving past or in the middle of a fight. A house
named by its number reads as a name: "go into 32 Jamestown St". The read me
says what ScriptHookVDotNet it needs: 3.6 or newer, as it has since 0.9.7.
