📍 POSTED UP A drug-dealing and gang mod for GTA V. You pick the corner, not the customer. You run with the Chamberlain Gangster Families. Buy product, cut it at home, stand somewhere busy and let the trade come to you. Then be gone before the police, or somebody else's people, decide you've been there long enough. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🔁 THE LOOP
  1. 📦 Get it. Gerald fronts you the first two packages. After that it's by the gram from him or by the brick at the port.
  2. 🧪 Cut it. Brick weight won't sell until you've cut it at the sink in Denise's kitchen. How hard you step on it is the only real decision in the mod. 100g at half strength is 200g to sell — and the block can tell.
  3. 📍 Post up. Pick a spot. Busy pavements sell fast and get you noticed fast. Every block pays differently.
  4. 🚨 Move on. Heat sticks to the ground, not to you. A new block is a clean start. The one you burned stays burned.

  • 🤝 Thirteen suppliers to track down;
  • 🎯 Six jobs from Lamar, who rides along and doesn't shut up;
  • 🚔 Police that aren't a wanted level. Patrols work your blocks regardless. Get searched holding product and it's gone;
  • 🏁 Street takeovers one night in three. Burnouts, a crowd, and they'll buy from you;
  • 🍺 A block that lives without you. Crews drinking and smoking, lowriders and donks cruising, dogs on leads, guards who take breaks;
  • 📱 A social feed of 174 people reacting to what you actually do;
  • 📉 Block saturation. Worked corners go quiet, then recover while you're away;
  • 🏠 Stash house raids. Hold too much and someone comes for it;
  • 💊 Seven products, and yes, you can take them yourself;
  • 🚗 Eleven cars from Hao, a cheap mod shop, driverless rides off the phone;
  • 🔫 Guns from Stretch, bodyguards, turf war, dead drops, and a spray can app called Overspray;
  • 👕 Six outfits at the closet, plus how Franklin walks and holds a gun;

  • 🎤 152 recorded lines. Gerald, Lamar, Tao, Hao and Stretch speak.
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🔧 NO DEPENDENCIES, NO GAME FILES TOUCHED One DLL and some JSON. No LemonUI, no NativeUI, no OpenIV, no installer. It won't fight your graphics mods, car packs or map edits. Uninstalling is deleting three things. Want to tinker? 11 JSON files and 103 settings in a commented INI. Legacy and Enhanced, same DLL. Single player only — keep it away from GTA Online. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 📦 REQUIREMENTS
  • Script Hook V;
  • ScriptHookVDotNet 3 (v2 alone won't do it; 3.6+ for Enhanced);
  • .NET Framework 4.8. Windows 10 and 11 already have it.
🤝 Compatibility. Graphics, map and texture mods (NVE, Forests, RWEU, Remastered packs) are fine — nothing here replaces a game file. Trainers are fine. Police mods like RDE run alongside; this mod's patrols still happen. Phone mods share the phone key, so rebind it in the INI. You don't need the standalone Overspray, the app does the same thing. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🛠️ INSTALL
  1. ⚠️ Unblock the zip first. Right-click → Properties → Unblock → OK. Windows marks downloaded files and ScriptHookVDotNet silently refuses a marked DLL. This is the number one reason it "doesn't work";
  2. 📁 Drag the scripts folder into your GTA V folder, the one with GTA5.exe in it. Merge when asked.
You'll end up with scripts\Hoodrich.dll, scripts\Hoodrich.ini and a scripts\Hoodrich\ folder. 🔄 Upgrading: drop it over the top, old saves load. Copy the folder as well as the DLL. 🗑️ Uninstalling: delete those three things. 🚫 No log at all? The mod writes its own log on startup, so if scripts\Hoodrich\Hoodrich.log doesn't exist the DLL never ran. That's almost always ScriptHookVDotNet 3 missing. Check your GTA folder for ScriptHookVDotNet3.dll. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ▶️ GETTING STARTED 🗺️ Gerald is the only marker on the map. See him at the flats on Grove and he'll hand you the first lot. Open the phone, go to Dealing, post up somewhere with people on it. 💡 The bit nobody works out on their own: weight you buy won't sell until it's cut and bagged at Denise's sink. Anything you're given is already bagged. 🎮 The phone button opens everything. Arrows move, Enter chooses, Backspace goes back, d-pad right talks to people. Every key is rebindable. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 📜 CHANGELOG 🧹 0.7.0 — 8 September 2026
