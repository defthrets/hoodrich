📍 POSTED UP A drug-dealing and gang mod for GTA V, built from scratch. You pick the corner, not the customer. You run with the Chamberlain Gangster Families. Get hold of product, take it home and cut it, stand somewhere busy and let the trade come to you — and try to be gone before the police or somebody else's people decide you have been there long enough. Everything runs off a phone that replaces the in-game one. Your weapon wheel is left exactly as it was. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ 🔁 THE LOOP
  1. 📦 Get hold of it. Gerald fronts you the first two packages for nothing, so you do not need money to start. After that you buy — off him by the gram, or off the man at the port by the brick, once you have proved you come back.
  2. 🧪 Cut it. Weight bought by the brick will not sell to anybody. It goes to the sink in Denise's kitchen first, and how hard you step on it is the only real decision in the mod. 100g at half strength is 200g to sell. It is also obviously weak, and the block can tell.
  3. 📍 Stand somewhere. You do not pick customers, you pick a spot. A busy pavement sells fast and gets you noticed fast; an empty street does neither. Buyers walk up and ask for an amount, and what one block pays is not what the next one pays.
  4. 🚨 Do not still be there. Heat builds where you stand and sticks to the ground, not to you. A different block is a clean start, and the one you burned is still burned when you come back.

  • 🏴 Nine gangs — Families, Ballas, Vagos, Aztecas, Marabunta Grande, Lost MC, Triads, Armenian Mob, Kkangpae. Named leaders standing on their own turf, their own colours, their own standing with you. Rep, bodies, money and beef are tracked per gang, not as one number;
  • 🤝 Thirteen suppliers to find — eight gang corners, three independents, plus Gerald and Tao at the port. A dealer is a man on a corner until you have stood in front of him;
  • 📈 A reputation separate from your rank — sell decent product and your name climbs. Step on everything and it does not, and a refusal is never just one lost sale. They remember, they tell people, and some of them want to do something about it;
  • 🎯 Six jobs from Lamar — a straightener at the Chamberlain courts, a drive-by through Jamestown, going over a rival's tags, a cut house in Rancho, getting the Ballas off Grove, and a route through La Mesa. He rides out with you, keeps up, and does not shut up;
  • 🚔 A police system that is not a wanted level — patrols work the blocks whether or not anybody is after you. Work one spot long enough and they stop driving past and start stopping. Searched holding product costs you the product; holding nothing costs you a fine, which is why dropping a bag before they reach you works;
  • 📉 Block saturation — every gram sold makes the next customer there slower to show, and it recovers while you are elsewhere;
  • 🏠 Stash house raids — hold too much and somebody comes for it while you are out;
  • 📱 A social feed of 174 people with fixed handles and avatars, and 1,664 template lines across 106 event sets, filled in with the block you are on, the set you run with and the money involved. Post up and the word goes out in a language that does not say anything outright;
  • 🏁 A street takeover, one night in three, at one of two junctions that take turns — cars burning out and spinning in the middle, a crowd stood on the pavement corners, and nothing placed anywhere: every car drives itself in. It happens whether you go or not, and if you post up at it the crowd buys from you;
  • 🍺 A block that is lived on when nothing is happening — crews walking the back streets with a beer or a cigarette, stopping for a drink and a drag, talking loud enough that you hear them before you see them. Lowriders one night and donks the next. Corners with people on them, a bike meet at Leroy's, and 🐕 a quarter of the crews walking a dog on a real rope lead;
  • 👕 Six outfits at the closet, hung up and named the way the game's own wardrobe does it, plus rows for how he walks and how he holds a gun;
  • 🚗 Serve out of the car window — post up in the driver's seat of a stopped car and they come to the door instead of the pavement;
  • 💊 Seven products, eleven cars from Hao, guns from Stretch — plus bodyguards, turf war, dead drops, a stash house, a low-end mod shop at under half the price of the one over the river, driverless cars off the phone, and a spray can with thirteen colours;
  • 😵 You can take your own product, and each of the seven does something different — weed slows the world, coke makes you fast and bright, meth is a high you can feel, and heroin is the one that ends with you on the ground. The second one is the one you notice; four in you and you wake up somewhere else;
  • 🎤 152 recorded lines — Gerald, Lamar, Tao Cheng, Hao and Stretch say what the screen says, and the ones nobody has recorded are read rather than heard.

  • 🚫 Zero external dependencies. No LemonUI, no NativeUI, no Newtonsoft, no Lua, no OpenIV, no asset replacement, no installer. One DLL and some JSON. Nothing in your scripts folder can lose a version fight with it, and no game file is modified;
  • 📝 Everything is data. 11 JSON files you can edit — drugs, prices, gangs, turf, dealers, missions, cars, weapons, scenery and every line of the social feed. Break one and the mod says so in the log and falls back to its built-in copy rather than failing to load. 102 settings in a commented INI; delete any line to go back to its default;
  • 🎨 107 icons, drawn from scratch for this mod. No Rockstar assets ship with it.

  1. ⚠️ Unblock the zip before extracting. Right-click it → Properties → tick Unblock → OK. Windows marks downloaded files and ScriptHookVDotNet silently refuses to load a marked DLL — the mod just is not there, with nothing in any log to explain it. This is the single most common reason it "doesn't work";
  2. 📁 Drag the scripts folder into your GTA V folder — the one with GTA5.exe in it, normally C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\Grand Theft Auto V. Say yes to merging.
You should end up with scripts\Hoodrich.dll, scripts\Hoodrich.ini and the scripts\Hoodrich\ folder beside them. That is the whole install — no game files are modified. 🔄 Upgrading? Drop it over the top, old saves load — but copy scripts\Hoodrich\ as well as the DLL. ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━ ▶️ GETTING STARTED 🗺️ Gerald is the only marker on your map. Go see him at the flats on Grove — he puts the first lot of product in your hand for free. Then open the phone, go to Dealing, and post up somewhere with people on it. 💡 The one thing nobody works out on their own: weight you buy will not sell until it has been cut and bagged at the sink in Denise's kitchen. Anything you are given is already bagged. 🎮 Controls: the phone button opens everything. Arrows move, Enter chooses, Backspace goes back. Right on the d-pad talks to people. Every key is rebindable in Hoodrich.ini. 🗑️ Uninstall: delete those three things. Posting up used to have one flat refusal in it: get out of the car first. Now you can do it sitting in the driver's seat of a stopped car, and the window winds down as you start. Buyers walk to the driver's door rather than to the bonnet — the mark is worked out from the car's own width, so they stand at the glass of a van and at the glass of a coupe rather than a metre off one of them. He keeps his seat through the handoff, using the game's own traffic-stop gesture: the one where a driver passes his licence out to an officer at the door. Same movement, different thing in the hand. Driving off packs it up, measured in metres rather than in speed, so shuffling forward to let somebody past is staying put. Six outfits at the closet, and how he carries himself. The wardrobe at Denise's could dress him and remember what he had on, and that was all — one look, and changing it lost the last one. There are six pegs on it now: hang up what you are wearing, name it or let it call itself Outfit 3, and put it back on whenever you are at the rail. Two more rows next to the body choose how he walks and how he holds a gun, including the game's own gangster walk and its gang shooting style, and the change lands as you scroll it. A dog on a lead. A quarter of the crews walking the back streets bring one with them — a pug, a rottweiler or a shepherd — and the lead is one of the game's own ropes, pinned between the dog's neck and the man's hand so it hangs and swings and goes taut on its own. Five more of the set's cars, and they are black or green and nothing else. A Greenwood, an Impaler LX, a Manana Custom, a Tahoma Coupe and a Vamos. Every car the set drove used to be the same green, which reads as a fleet rather than a set; half are metallic black now, all of them on dark smoke glass, dropped springs and a silver pearl, with no other mods on any of them. Nobody of ours shoots at the police. Everyone of the set carries, so one patrol rolling past a hangout was the whole night gone. Anybody wearing one of the mod's gang groups who picks a fight with an officer is taken out of it and leaves instead, which is what people on a corner actually do. The two homies riding with you are the exception and only half of it: they stop shooting and they do not run, because running would leave you in the car. Three things an audit found that had never worked. The crews on foot could not be told to stand down — the switch was there, its comment said the house script wired it, and the house script never did, so they kept turning up through raids and takeovers. Lamar's tag job never wrote a tag: the glyph table, the layout and the word-picker were all correct and none of them was ever called, so every tag in the game has been the random splatter that is meant to be the fallback. And two gangs were drawn the wrong colour on the map, both cyan gangs sitting on the blue blip. Smaller things. Gerald is off the ecstasy and stops advertising an uncut lot nobody can buy. Nothing at a takeover fits a wheelie bar any more, and a boot held open has speakers in it. The Asbo on the pad at Lamar's, on competition springs. The car by the decks is a Buccaneer Custom. Franklin's driving is back to vanilla — the window only comes down when he is serving. 🩹 0.6.1 — 7 September 2026 Two fixes off the first day of reports. The phone stopped working once you got to the safe house, and so did the kitchen: you could not call Tao Cheng and the counter would not answer. Walking into Franklin's runs the game's own house scripts — the bed, the wardrobe, the television — and any of them can raise the mission flag and then leave it raised while you potter about indoors. This mod stands down while that flag is up, which is the right thing to do on a story mission and the wrong thing to do in your own kitchen. From where you are standing it looks like the mod simply died, with nothing on screen to say why. The flag no longer stands it down inside the house. Every other reason still does — the controls being taken off you, a cutscene, a faded screen, the pause menu. If you are staying on 0.6.0, PauseDuringMission=false in the ini works around it. The social feed can be turned off on screen, which it could not be before. The old setting only ever chose which side of the screen a post landed on; this one means neither. Nothing is drawn, nothing goes into the game's own notification stack, and so nothing chimes at you either. The timeline still fills in the background, so every post is there to read in the phone whenever you want it — it just stops narrating itself while you play. There are three ways to work it: FeedOnScreen under [Socials] in Hoodrich.ini, a tick on the mod's own settings screen, and a key — NumPad * by default — that toggles it where you stand and writes the answer back to the ini, so it survives a restart.
