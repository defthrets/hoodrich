THE HUNT GETS A COMPASS, AND THEY STOP STANDING STILL. The ring said where he
was to within forty metres and the trail said where he went, and neither was
any use with your head up. The card carries a compass now: which way each of
them is from where you are looking. Before you have his trail the mark is wide
and dim and sits on the ring's slipped centre; once you have stood on his
prints it goes narrow and green and follows the man. And they walk -- a fresh
spot every seven to fifteen seconds instead of one move every minute, so there
is a trail to read at all. Spooking one now ends the job there and then,
rather than leaving a count that could never be finished.

PILLS GO DOWN, A TAB GOES ON THE TONGUE, AND THE NEEDLE ENDS ON THE GROUND. A
ritual can have a second act. Xanax and ecstasy are the one pill clip the game
has, cut at the swallow, the bottle in the other hand, and a bottle of water
after it. LSD is the same hand to the mouth, cut as the tab goes in. Heroin
ends slumped on his side on the ground rather than in a ragdoll.

THE CARS WE PUT ON THE STREET BURN NO FUEL. The two at Lamar's party hold
their engines and radios on all night, so Running on Fumes emptied their tanks
and cut the engines and the next tick turned them back on, for as long as you
stood there. The party, the takeover's kerb, the car meet, the cruise and the
rollers all say they are scenery now. Get in one and it is a car again.

THE LOCKER STOPS ANNOUNCING ITSELF. Guns coming back after a load is the mod
keeping a promise, not news.
