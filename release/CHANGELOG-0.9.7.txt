0.9.7 -- 18 September 2026


IT LOADS ON ANY SCRIPTHOOKVDOTNET 3. The mod was compiled against the 3.9.0
fork, the compiler stamps that number into the dll, and the loader will not
run a script that asks for a newer ScriptHookVDotNet than itself -- so 3.6,
every 3.7 nightly, and anything that was not the fork got either a bare load
failure or "MissingMethodException: PostTicker" with the mod switched off.
That was the commonest report on the page under half a dozen names. It is
built against 3.6.0 now, the way Fumes is, and everything that only existed
in 3.9 is on the older name or on the native underneath. You do not need
3.9.0.0 any more; whatever ScriptHookVDotNet 3 you have will do.


THE MARK THAT DROVE OFF ON SOMEBODY ELSE'S CAR. His Buffalo and his bike
were marked with a blip attached to the vehicle, and the game streams a
vehicle out without telling its blip -- the handle goes to whatever spawns
next, and his car's mark was on a stranger's Sentinel doing forty down
Vespucci. The same on two wheels: a bike that "spawned in with a green blip"
was a traffic bike that had been handed the old handle. One blip on the map
now, placed by coordinate and moved with the car, attached to nothing.


THE CAR THAT VANISHED OFF HAO'S LOT. Buy one, put your own plate on it, and
some of them were gone when you came back -- the Toros and the Sentinel in
the report. The lot keeps a handle for each display car and trusted it as
long as it still existed; a handle the game had recycled onto the car you
had just bought passed that test, so the lot counted it as stock and deleted
it with the stock when you walked off. Every display car carries its sale
plate from the day it is put out, and the lot deletes nothing it cannot
prove is its own by model and plate. The green sale marker is gone as well:
it was a second blip attached to the car, and the keys OwnedCars puts on it
were already there.


THE BIKE SWAP WAS TAKING ANY BAGGER. Franklin's Bagger becomes the bike in
the ini, and so did every Bagger with nobody on it inside a hundred and
twenty metres of him -- the Lost's, the ones the game parks. Only the one on
his own drive is his.


THE WAR DOES NOT START ON A FULL BLOCK. The carloads and the defenders held
off once the world was full, and that was the whole of the protection -- so
a war could still open at two hundred and seventeen peds, put three cars
and twelve men down before the first hold-off fired, and take the game down
with it. That is kocabac's log, every line of it saying FULL from the one
that announces the war. It is not started while the world is over the line
now, it fights out of two car models rather than nine (ERR_MEM_EMBEDDEDALLOC
is a streaming pool, and variety is what fills it), and the lines are
yours: [Block] PedsBusy and CarsBusy in Hoodrich.ini. Lower them if a busy
block is crashing you; the log says what the counts were.


THE BAG LANDS ON THE GROUND. It came off where you died, and dying mid-air
put that in the sky -- off a bike over the hills, out of a helicopter -- so
the bag was frozen fifty metres over the blip or under the slope, and gone.
It goes on the ground under where you died now, or where you last stood
when there is nothing under you, a placed bag is checked against the floor
before it is frozen, and a lost one can be brought to your feet from the
settings screen: "Bring my bag here". [Dealing] BagProp names any prop for
the bag on the floor.


THE PHONE BUTTON IS YOURS TO KEEP. [Phone] PhoneButton=false in Hoodrich.ini
gives the up arrow back to the game -- Franklin's own phone comes up on it
like it always did -- and only the key (F2 by default) opens this one. For
anybody whose other mods live on the arrow keys.


AND HIS CAR CAN BE LEFT ALONE. [Cars] FranklinsCar= turns the Buffalo swap
off, the way FranklinsBike= always could. Blank leaves the game's own car.
