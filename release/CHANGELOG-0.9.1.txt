0.9.1 -- 14 September 2026


THE FIXES 0.9.0 ANNOUNCED ARE ACTUALLY IN IT NOW. Three of the headline lines
in the last release went out on the page and not in the zip -- the drug
durations, meth, and heroin were all written down after the download was cut.
Anybody who installed 0.9.0 got the old numbers and the nod. This is the
release where the words and the file agree.

  Every high lasts twice as long. Weed a hundred seconds to two hundred,
  cocaine five minutes to ten, heroin seven to fourteen. The comedown after
  them is untouched -- the wreckage is the price, not the effect.

  Meth is speed and nothing that slows you down. Forty per cent quicker on
  his feet, and no drunk stagger or seven-eighths world speed underneath it
  making him wade. The hallucinations stay where they were: they wait for the
  second dose, which is what a higher dose is for.

  Heroin no longer puts you on the floor. It chained into the nod -- on your
  side for the minutes after, which is honest about the drug and a long time
  to spend looking at the back of your own head with no way to get up.


EVERY MAN GIVES HIS OWN WORK. Vernon's job was filed on Lamar's board, so
Lamar offered you a basement he has never been in and Vernon stood outside his
own shop with nothing to say. Each giver now offers only what is his: Lamar's
list is Lamar's, Vernon's kilo is Vernon's, and the port is Gerald's.


THE SHORT BRIEF STOPS BEING A PREFACE TO THE LONG ONE. Coming back to a job
you had already done played the one-line version and then the full brief on
top of it, which read as a man forgetting he had just spoken.


THE HUNT: STABBING A MAN NO LONGER COUNTS AS BEING SEEN. He shouted that he
had spotted you with a knife already in his neck, and blew the hunt for it.
And the man you put down stays on the ground he fell on instead of sinking to
his waist in it.


"GET 'EM OFF GROVE" IS GONE. It is off Lamar's board rather than replaced;
what goes in its place is a separate decision and will arrive as one.
