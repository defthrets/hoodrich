0.9.4 -- 15 September 2026


FOUR FAULTS IN 0.9.3, ALL OF THEM MINE, ALL FOUND BY READING IT BACK RATHER
THAN BY ANYBODY HAVING TO PLAY IT.


THE BLOCK WOULD HAVE GONE QUIET. 0.9.3 put a world-is-full check on every
ambient spawner, which was the right fix for the crashes -- and the ceiling
for VEHICLES was set at 140 on no evidence at all. Four hundred and fifty
four samples out of the log say cars average 137 and sit at or above 140 for
fifty-seven per cent of them. More than half the time, every spawner in the
mod would have stood down: empty corners, takeovers that never fill, meets
that never happen. That is worse than the crash it was added to stop, and it
looks exactly like the mod being broken.

230 now, which is above the ninety-ninth percentile of ordinary play and
below the worst ever seen. Peds were checked the same way and 190 was already
right -- they average 99 and reach it one time in a hundred.


AND THE MEET WOULD HAVE GIVEN UP ON ITSELF. A car meet abandons a parking
space after four failed sends and moves to the next one, which is right when
the space is the problem. "The world is full this second" is not the space's
fault and passes on its own -- but it was being counted as a miss, so a busy
spell would have burned through every space in the meet, four tries at a
time, and given up on the whole thing. The check happens before the counter
now.


THE REAL PHONE WOULD HAVE BEEN TAKEN OFF YOU. The fix for both phones opening
at once ended the game's handset whenever this mod's phone was AVAILABLE,
rather than only while it was open -- which is most of the time you are
walking about. A story call that is ringing rather than connected would have
gone with it. Two phones at once can only happen while ours is up, so that is
the only time it happens now.


AND THE DOGS STOPPED. Asking the game for a model no longer stops the script,
which is what took the seconds of dark HUD out of 0.9.3 -- and the dog a
walking crew is rolled is asked for exactly once, at the moment the crew is
made. So the first attempt now finds the model still loading and the dog is
never created at all. The crew remembers it is owed one and keeps asking for
eight seconds, which is what the waiting used to buy by standing there.


Everything else in 0.9.3 stands: the ammo that read zero after a reload, both
phones opening, the fridge that said nothing, the spawn budget behind the
crashes, the models that no longer stop the script, Vernon, the comedown, the
walks and the inventory.
