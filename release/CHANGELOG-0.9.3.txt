0.9.3 -- 15 September 2026


THE THINGS YOU REPORTED

  Bought ammunition read ZERO after a reload. Two faults together: the count
  was written down before the locker had handed the guns back, so it recorded
  whatever the base game had -- nothing -- and a count of zero dropped the
  whole ROW from the save rather than storing a zero, so one autosave in the
  first six seconds erased the rounds for good. Both fixed. Thanks gaspin12.

  Both phones opened at once. Hiding the cellphone HUD component is not
  closing a phone: the game's own script carried on underneath with its own
  handset. It is properly ended now, and again if anything raises it while
  ours is up. Thanks Vorx4643.

  Standing at the fridge did nothing. The house tells you where product goes
  on your first visit and never again -- so coming home on your third night
  with full pockets, in a kitchen with a fridge in it, nothing said a word.
  It says it again when you walk in CARRYING something. Thanks NordCyborg.

  Crashes and frame drops around busy blocks. The mod has always had a
  world-is-full check and exactly one system consulted it. Thirty others
  spawned people and cars without ever asking. Every ambient spawner asks
  now -- the takeover and its crowd, car meets, rollers, cruises, walkers,
  block life, corner crews, drive-bys and patrols -- and vehicles are counted
  as well as people, which is the pool that was actually running out. Missions
  and anything you personally called for are untouched. Thanks kocabac and
  Felony83.


AND THE MOD STOPS DROPPING FRAMES OF ITS OWN

  Asking the game for a model USED TO STOP THE SCRIPT. Every fixture, crew and
  walker waited up to 1.2 seconds per model with the whole mod's HUD switched
  off -- three and a half seconds of dark screen to put a crate down on a
  corner, every time you walked back to it. They ask and come back now.

  The graffiti bystander scan did a ped sweep and six raycasts inside one
  frame, 284 times in a nine hour session. The same six looks are spread over
  half a second instead.


VERNON

  He texts you after the buy, and he sells. Powder to the house at a price
  nobody else in the mod does -- under the port -- and two kilos a call,
  because he is a man with a basement rather than a dock. The text arrives
  once you have driven away, not while he is stood in front of you.

  His messages have his face on them. He is a story ped with no contact
  picture in the game at all, so his is photographed.

  Kill him on his own job and the job ends. It could not have finished
  anyway: the hand-in is walking up to Vernon.


DRUGS

  The comedown is ONE washed out grey for two minutes, and the camera stops
  swaying through it. It was four pictures, two of them changing under you,
  with a shake running the whole time -- which on top of running and turning
  read as the screen tearing. The numbers are milder too: it was a window in
  which anything that picked a fight with you won it.

  Acid warps properly. The colours are untouched; it is the world that moves.

  The bars and heroin walk like cocaine on the first one -- which is to say
  not at all, just quicker -- and like meth on the double.


AND

  Die with the bag on and you drop it, with everything in it. Die without it
  and your pockets are simply gone: the bag is the walk back now, rather than
  both being the same walk.

  The inventory is laid out like Bare Minimum's pocket -- one grid, five
  across, tiles twice the size, in a panel a third of the screen instead of
  over half.
