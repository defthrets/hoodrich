## 0.9.5 — everything since 0.8.1

**vernon's got a job.** he walks you down to the basement, then a kilo off an armenian
at rogers scrap. his dorado's parked down the side of the shop. finish it and he sells
you powder to the house, cheaper than the port.

**a bag you can lose.** twenty slots, worn on your back. die with it on and you drop it
with everything in it. die without it and your pockets are gone.

**inventory redone** — one grid, bigger tiles, laid out like bare minimum's pocket.

**drugs** — everything lasts twice as long. meth is speed now, not a stagger. heroin
doesn't put you on the floor. acid warps properly. the comedown is one grey wash for
two minutes instead of four effects and a shaking camera.

**hunt got a compass**, fam app got three pages, and the whole voice pack is levelled.

**your reports** — ammo reading zero after a reload (gaspin12), both phones opening at
once (Vorx4643), the fridge doing nothing (NordCyborg), crashes and frame drops on busy
blocks (kocabac, Felony83). all fixed.

**performance** — loading a model was stopping the whole script for up to a second at a
time with the HUD off. that's gone.
