0.9.11 -- 4 October 2026


FIVE MORE CORNERS, one to a set. Pelon for the Vagos, on a corner in Cypress
Flats: blow and glass. Nelson for Marabunta Grande, on a bench up in El Burro
Heights: glass and blow. Jae for the Kkangpae, on a corner in Little Seoul,
the same two. Vahe for the Armenians, by the freeway in La Puerta: brown and
nothing else. OG Reese for the Ballas, on a corner in Davis: rock and green.
All five sell in person and nowhere else, cheap and stepped on to three
quarters -- buy a big enough lot and it comes clean. Each is cold the first
time, the way Flaco is: say what you came for, keep it about the money, and
he sells. That first buy squares you with his set on their own blocks, and no
further than that. Three of his people stand round him.


EVERY CORNER MAN IS ON THE MAP FROM THE FIRST LOAD, in his set's colour and
dimmed; meeting him brightens the pin. The Kkangpae are dark blue now, on the
map and on their cars.


LAMAR'S YARD, AS HE LEFT IT. The hanging weed has come down. A second wall, a
skate ramp, a couple of containers and a couch have gone up along the east
side.


TEXTURES GOING SOFT. Every model the mod ever asked the streamer for stayed
loaded for the rest of the session -- a hundred and thirty-odd cars and faces
from the first takeover alone -- and on a card with less memory than that, the
street turned to mud. A model nobody has used in twenty seconds is let go now.
A takeover parks twelve cars of six kinds instead of fourteen of fourteen, and
its crowd wears fourteen faces instead of forty-six. A big scene -- Parkview's
flats -- goes up nearer, so it is not sat in memory from two streets away.


STRETCH'S GUNS KEEP THEIR ROUNDS, PROPERLY. Two guns of one calibre share one
count, and a load handed both back with it -- so a pistol and a combat pistol
came back with double the rounds between them. They come back with what they
had. Rounds bought off Stretch for a gun you did not buy off him are kept the
same way now, and a scope or a grip fitted a minute before a save is still on
the gun after it.


WITH NPC MIND INSTALLED. The dealers, the gang leaders, Hao, Vernon and
Stretch have conversations of their own, and NPC Mind was opening its panel
over them with a stranger's name on it. They are this mod's people; it leaves
them alone. A pill sold through NPC Mind's talk is a whole pill at the price
of one, the way the corner sells them.


TRANSLATIONS. Every string the eight other languages were missing since 0.9.9,
and the old ones read through in full and fixed: Latin American Spanish
throughout, and the lines that said the opposite of what they meant. Notices
glued together out of pieces translate as a whole now.


FIXES. A dealer who only sells at his door no longer hands you a number he
will not answer. For whoever records voices: a greeting, a truce or a doorstep
line with no recording is named in the log like every other missing line, so
the to-record list is complete, and voice_lines.py lists exactly what each man
can say.
