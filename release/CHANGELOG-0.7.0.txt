0.7.0 -- 8 September 2026


THE GROW ROOM AND THE PILL PRESS ARE GONE. Both doors, both markers, both
blips. They were freezing the game for people going in and out, and four
rounds of work never proved the rooms were even there. The freeze itself is
fixed at the root as well -- a wait that could spin forever if the game
paused mid-fade -- so any door you add through the ini can't hang either.

THE MOD STOPS DELETING THE WORLD WHEN ONE THING GOES WRONG. A single caught
error used to tear down every scene, car and person the mod had placed and
rebuild them the next frame -- the "everything blinked out" moment. Now it
just carries on. Every deprecated ScriptHookVDotNet call is replaced too,
so it runs on newer SHVDN builds, and every native it uses was checked
against SHVDN's own table.

STRETCH'S COUNTER. Extended mags actually stay on now -- the locker was
quietly putting the stock clip back over them after a death, which is why
guns came back with a suppressor and no mag. X on a pad opens the parts, as
the bar said it would. And the parts have their own pictures.

YOUR PEOPLE AND THE POLICE. The Families never start a fight with an
officer, but they finish one the officer starts.

THE BLOCK FEELS LIVED IN. Guards put the gun down for a smoke or a drink
now and then, never in step with each other. Everyone at Lamar's uses the
furniture. The meet on the court is a crowd rather than a ring -- they
drift, and one of them films it. Dog walkers: the lead runs hand to head,
pays out with the distance, and snaps at twenty metres; the dog keeps pace
instead of being dragged.

TRIGGER. He runs when you run. His prompt is the mod's own line along the
bottom instead of the game's help box. Green blip. TriggerBreed in the ini,
with proper petting for eleven breeds.

XANAX. One bar can put you on the floor -- rarely, about once a minute
moving -- and the drunk walk comes and goes instead of sticking for the
whole high.

SMALLER. The graffiti app is called Overspray and wears its wordmark; you
don't need the standalone alongside. The police radio recording is gone.
The Asbo has its speakers. Two riflemen on the roof at Lamar's. WalkerDogs
and a higher WalkerCrews cap in the ini. Compiler warnings from thirty-six
to none.
