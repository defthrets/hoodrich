0.9.5 -- 15 September 2026


VERNON STOPS ASKING YOU TO DO A JOB YOU HAVE ALREADY DONE. Walking up to him
after the buy put him straight back into pitching it, and the cause was that
the offer read a flag about a DOOR: the basement on Strawberry is locked
behind "vernon_intro", and the settings screen has a switch reading "Lock
Leroy's basement again" which takes that flag off. One flag, two jobs -- lock
the door and the mission is un-completed.

He pitches on two occasions now and no others: you have never taken it, or the
last go ended badly. Death, arrest and Vernon being killed all travel the same
road, so all three put him back to asking; handing the package over settles it.
Neither flag has anything to do with a door and neither can be toggled from a
menu.


TWO MEN STOP TALKING AT ONCE. The chatter facial is a looping animation and it
runs until something is played over it -- so when Ardo started, Vernon carried
on mouthing away underneath him, and a conversation between two people looked
like two people ignoring each other. The last mouth is closed on the handover
now.


AND VERNON STOPS GETTING STUCK IN THE YARD. He is handed a combat task when the
shooting starts and nothing ever took it off him, so with every Armenian on the
floor he stood there running a fight against nobody -- and the follow task
could not get a word in, because combat outranks it. He is taken out of it when
the last one goes down, and his reactions are switched back off with it: a yard
full of fresh corpses kept handing him things to react to that cancelled
whatever he had just been told.


UNDERNEATH: failing a job no longer starts the ten minute wait before Lamar has
anything. That was never intended -- it came of filing a "this went wrong" note
in the same place as "this is finished" -- and it meant failing a job was
punished twice.


AND THE BUILD CAN NO LONGER LIE ABOUT A RELEASE. On 0.9.4 one of the two
downloads failed to upload on a network timeout and the script printed
"Published, both attached" anyway, because nothing ever checked. Uploads are
retried and then the release page itself is asked what it actually has.
