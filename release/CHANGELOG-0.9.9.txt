0.9.9 -- 26 September 2026


PARKVIEW IS PART OF POSTED UP. The block on the open ground in Chamberlain
Hills -- two apartment blocks and a row of houses, a corner shop with its two
Koreans behind the counter, a camp at the hobo spot, and eighty-odd people on
the roofs, the floors and the ground -- ships inside Posted Up now rather than
as a mod of its own. If you had Parkview.dll, take it out of scripts: your
rented room comes across on the first start. The people there live a little:
they change what they are doing, walk off and come back, and walk in from off
screen rather than appearing on their marks. And every person this mod puts
on a street can be talked to with NPC Mind.


THE GAMBLING DEN. Behind the die on the ground at Parkview, in a two-car garage
under Pillbox Hill: roulette with the ball on the wheel, blackjack dealt card
by card, and pokies at $10 a spin, the Diamond's own tables and screens, with
dealers who talk while you play, guards on the door, and the camera free to
look about while you are sat down. The tables' limits are in Hoodrich.ini
under [GamblingDen].


APARTMENT E2. A second place to rent at Parkview, $500 a week: the door up on
the landing. Inside, a grow in the corner, a kitchen bench to cut at, a couch
to sleep on, and the stash. The apartments' icons are white until they are
yours and green once they are. The room Parkview already rented is a stash
house now too, and every stash house holds the one stock.


THE PLUG COMES TO PARKVIEW. Order on the phone while you are at Parkview and
the dealer pulls up on the block, walks up to wherever you are standing,
throws it at your feet and goes. Pick it up and it is in your stash.


SHOOTING HOOPS. The basketball court in Chamberlain Hills has a ball on it:
walk into the ring in the middle of the court and pick it up. Hold to charge
the throw -- the line out of the ball rises and stretches with the meter, and
fades before it comes down, so where it lands is yours to judge -- and down
through either hoop is two, or three from beyond the arc. Nothing aims it for
you, and you can throw it as far as you like. A setting draws the rings the
ball has to go through, to practise with. The same court is out as a mod of
its own, Hoops; with Posted Up installed it stands down.


LAMAR'S BIKE RIDE ends on the basketball court now, where the Ballas are
waiting.


THE BLOCK. The set rides round Parkview in crews -- on BMXs, dirt bikes and
quads, pulling wheelies and burnouts -- along a way round the park that was
ridden by hand, instead of through everything standing on it, and some crews
go round it the other way. The takeover's cars skid for real, on the physics,
and keep their tyres spinning until they are off course, and they crash like
cars rather than ploughing through everything. The car meet on the lot off
Davis is gone, the takeover throws fewer flares, and the kickabout from 0.9.8
is out.


THE FEED, REWRITTEN. Every GTA character in it was looked up and written from
their own story; the gang voices post the way young men on a block actually
post, swearing included; twenty-four ordinary working people join the city;
and the chatter knows what time it is, what the weather is doing and which
part of the map you are in. Each account types like one person rather than
the same careful writer seventy times over.


WHAT HE WEARS STAYS ON HIM. The outfit from the closet holds through death,
Pillbox, a cell and loading a save.


STRETCH stands on the ground in his yard rather than a metre over it, keeps
what you bought from him, and sells drum magazines as standard.


WITH OTHER MODS. Going through a body belongs to Bare Minimum now, and the bag
on the floor is whichever one it has on your back. Five0 Patrol's search takes
what it finds, and sees uncut product as well as bagged. Another mod can buy
off you and hand you what it found, at the corner's own prices.


THE SETTINGS ARE WHAT YOU SET. The settings screen and Hoodrich.ini hold only
what somebody playing would change, each row and line saying what it does; the
tuning numbers and the tools for building the mod are gone from both. Delete a
line and it goes back to the default. An ini you already have is left alone.
The mod's own doors are data now, in Hoodrich\doors.ini.


FIXES. Buildings placed as props no longer blink out in the distance, and the
map no longer drops to its low-detail models round Parkview. People in scenes
stop falling through floors and off roofs as a save loads. The phone no longer
flickers while something waits for a model to load. The house stops telling
you that you are carrying. The man on the ledge behind Innocence sits on it.
