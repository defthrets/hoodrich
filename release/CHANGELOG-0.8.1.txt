A FOLDER OF DOWNLOADED MAPS NO LONGER KILLS THE SCRIPT ON LOAD. Reading
Menyoo's spooner folder happened in one tick, and one player's folder --
sixty-odd downloaded maps, hospitals of fifteen hundred props, haunted cities
reaching five kilometres -- held it for five seconds, which is
ScriptHookVDotNet's timeout, so the script was killed and it looked like a
crash. Menyoo's folder is not read at all now unless [Scenery] FromMenyoo is
turned on -- it was only ever a shortcut for making scenes -- and when it is,
the files are read a slice of a frame at a time and anything reaching past
250 m or holding more than 200 things is skipped with a line in the log. The
port van's model is asked for rather than waited for, so no single tick can
reach the line.

YOUR GUNS COME BACK LOADED. The locker handed a bought gun back empty after a
load, and the first thing anybody saw was a trip to Stretch for rounds they
already had. The count is written down with the gun at every save and comes
back with it; a gun you still have is topped up once, on the first look after
a load, and never again.

THE PHONE'S GRID IS PIXEL ART, AND THE PRODUCT IS TOO. Every app tile is a
flipbook split out of a GIF: grey and still on the grid, in colour and moving
under the cursor. The Mask app is off the grid; the balaclava, the shop and
the setting stay. Weed, coke, crack, meth, heroin, ecstasy, LSD and Xanax
draw the pixel pictures Bare Minimum carries, the same file in both mods.
