PARKVIEW ROOMS
==============
The doors you can rent a room behind. One per line:

    X, Y, Z, heading, name

X Y Z is the spot you stand to be offered the room -- the doorway itself, read
off the coordinate HUD standing in front of it. Heading is which way you face
when you come back out. Everything after the fourth comma is the name, and it
is what the prompt and the feed call the room.

A line starting with # is ignored, and so is anything that is not five fields.
Add a door by standing at it and writing the line; there is no limit.

ONLY B2 IS LET. Michael asked for the one door on 2026-09-22 -- the barred one on
the ground floor, north side. The other five are kept below as comments rather
than deleted, because the coordinates were read off the HUD door by door and are
a nuisance to get again: take the # off one and it is rentable from that load.

THE ROOM ITSELF is not in here -- it is one spot for all of them, and it lives
in rooms.sav next to the log, because you set it in game rather than by hand.
Stand in the room you want and press the set-room key (Alt+F9 by default).
Until it is set, the mod uses the motel interior it ships with, and says so in
the log the first time anybody tries a door.
