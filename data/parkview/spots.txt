PARKVIEW SPOTS
==============
Things you can do standing somewhere. One per line, four fields separated by | :

    X,Y,Z | radius | action | label

Radius is how near you have to be stood, in metres. Action is one of:

    sleep      sleep until morning -- Parkview's own, works on its own
    wardrobe   Posted Up's wardrobe
    cut        Posted Up's cook screen, for cutting and bagging

A line starting with # is ignored, and so is anything without three bars.
