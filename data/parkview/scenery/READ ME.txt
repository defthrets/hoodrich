OBJECT SPOONER SCENES
=====================

Anything you arrange in Menyoo's Object Spooner can be built by the mod every session
instead of only in the session you placed it.

THE SCENES IN THIS FOLDER NEED NOTHING. They ship with the mod, they are read from here
whatever the settings say about Menyoo, every model and scenario in them is the game's
own, and a model an install does not have is left out and named in Parkview.log rather
than stopping anything. Menyoo is only needed to MAKE a scene, never to see one.

HOW
---
1. Place your peds, cars and props in Menyoo's Object Spooner as usual.
2. Object Spooner -> Saved Files -> save it under any name.
   Menyoo writes it to  <game folder>\menyooStuff\Spooner\<name>.xml
3. Set FromMenyoo=true in Parkview.ini, or copy the file in here. A file in here wins
   over one of the same name in Menyoo's folder.

Or press F9 stood in the middle of what you built: everything placed within 80 m is
written to a scene file in this folder, at its live position, and listed in the log.
That is the one thing that survives a crash.

THE NOTE
--------
A scene file's Note can carry instructions, one per line or separated by |:

  absorbs: a, b        the spooner saves this file was made from, skipped in Menyoo's
                       folder so a thing taken out does not come back
  sometimes 60: 1, 2   those saved handles are only there six days in ten
  stays: 1, 2          those peds never move off their marks -- somebody in bed: no
                       strolls, no walk-offs, never gone for the night. He is only
                       ever put on his mark while nobody can see it
  straight: 1, 2       those peds stand on a floor the game's paths do not know -- a
                       balcony, a roof, a shop or a yard walled in with props. They
                       walk in, stroll and walk off along their own floor, in
                       straight lines probed with rays, and never take a path
  roams 80:            with NOTHING after the colon, everybody the file does not say
                       stays. With handles after it, only those. The opposite of
                       stays: they live here rather than stand in it --
                       they walk anywhere within eighty metres of their mark instead
                       of the usual twelve, take a turn with the bins, couches and
                       stoves out there the way a hobo does, and barely stop moving.
                       Some of them walk drunk
  outings: X,Y,Z@14    somewhere worth leaving the place for, milled about within
                       fourteen metres of it: a roamer walks up now and then, dances
                       or smokes or talks a couple of minutes, and walks home. Six
                       away at once at most. A coordinate, not a scene name -- it can
                       be somewhere another mod built
  keeps: 1, db_apart_02_
                       never taken down when you walk away, and found again where
                       they were next time -- for the slow ones. A NUMBER is one
                       saved handle; a NAME is every placement of that model, which
                       is the easier way and survives re-saving the scene. The floor
                       tiles under a kept slab are kept with it. Sparingly: what is
                       kept is never let go of
  floors: 1@30.85      lay an invisible floor under that prop, top at that height, for
                       a model that has no collision of its own (the log names them)
  idles: 1=A,B; 2=C    what a particular man does, scenarios or animations as dict/clip,
                       rotated through by his beats

MOVING A VANILLA BENCH, LIGHT OR BIN
------------------------------------
It does not stick, and no scene file can make it. A bench on the street is
part of the MAP, not something somebody placed; Menyoo will shift the one in
front of you and it looks moved, but nothing in the map changed, so the next
time the block streams out and back it is at its old spot again. A scene file
only ever ADDS things.

So take it over instead: look at it and press Numpad *. The map one is hidden
for good (it goes into hidden.xml) and a real prop of the same model is put in
the same place, rotation and all. THAT one is a thing rather than a picture --
move it wherever you like, press F9, and it is in the scene where you left it.

DO IT BEFORE YOU MOVE IT. The hide is a sphere round where the prop is when you
press the key, so one you have already dragged ten metres leaves its map twin
sat at the old spot, outside the sphere. Put it back first, or point at the twin
and use the hide key on it.

TAKING SOMETHING OUT
--------------------
Look straight at a map prop and press Ctrl+F9: it goes, and is written into hidden.xml
in this folder so it stays gone. It will not take one of the scene's own props, or a
person, or a car. A scene file may also carry a Removals block by hand:

  <Removals><Remove><ModelHash>0x...</ModelHash><X>..</X><Y>..</Y><Z>..</Z><Radius>1.5</Radius></Remove></Removals>

THE PEOPLE
----------
They arrive on foot when you can see their marks, and are held frozen until the ground
under them is solid. Every three quarters of a minute to two and a half, one changes what
he is doing, has a word with whoever is near, throws a sign, stretches his legs, or walks
off out of sight and is back later, walking in. They talk within earshot. Hobos go
through the bins, wrecks and piles round them. Between QuietFrom and QuietTo on the
game's clock they drift off and drift back after. PedsLive=false in the ini leaves
everybody stood on his mark.

SETTINGS  (Parkview.ini, [Scenery])
-----------------------------------
Enabled     = true    Build saved scenes at all.
FromMenyoo  = false   Read menyooStuff\Spooner as well as this folder.
Range       = 220     How near you have to be, in metres.
PedsLive    = true    The people come and go.
QuietFrom   = 3       The hours, on the game's clock, that nobody is about.
QuietTo     = 7       The same number in both means never.
