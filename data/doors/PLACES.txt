===============================================================================
  POSTED UP  --  EVERYWHERE WITH AN INSIDE
===============================================================================


Every place in the game that has an inside worth a door, and what has to load
for it. The names came out of the string table of the interior loader on this
machine. Not one of them was typed from memory.

WHAT IS NOT IN HERE IS A SINGLE COORDINATE. A coordinate somebody typed has
been wrong every time this mod has tried it, and the grow room cost four
rounds of exactly that. So the mod holds the names and you supply the two
numbers, by standing on them.

HOW: settings screen, under "Doors". Pick the part of town, pick the place,
stand at its door and press "The door is here". Walk inside, stand where you
want to come out, press "...and I come out here". Press Insert. The door is
there, with a blip and a prompt, the same as the two rooms the mod ships.

A place marked ONLINE does not exist in a story game at all -- the door
switches the map over on the way in and back on the way out. A place marked
IN THE MAP is a mission room that has been there the whole time with no way
in; it needs the two presses and nothing else, so those are the easy ones.

Getting inside to mark the inside is the awkward half. The interior loader
already sitting in scripts\New folder will teleport you into any of these if
you put it back where it runs.
