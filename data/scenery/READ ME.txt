OBJECT SPOONER SCENES
=====================

Anything you arrange in Menyoo's Object Spooner can be built by the mod every
session instead of only in the session you placed it.

THE SCENES IN THIS FOLDER NEED NOTHING. They ship with the mod, they are read
from here whatever the settings say about Menyoo, every model and scenario in
them is the game's own, and a model an install does not have is left out and
named in Hoodrich.log rather than stopping anything. Menyoo is only needed to
MAKE a scene, never to see one.

HOW
---
1. Place your peds, cars and props in Menyoo's Object Spooner as usual.
2. Object Spooner -> Saved Files -> save it under any name.
   Menyoo writes it to  <game folder>\menyooStuff\Spooner\<name>.xml
3. That is all. The mod reads that folder as well as this one, so the scene is
   built from then on -- streamed in when you come near it and taken out again
   when you are well past.

Copy a file in HERE instead when you want it to travel with the mod, and it
wins over one of the same name in Menyoo's folder.

They came out of the spooner and were copied in here, so they no longer need
Menyoo installed. If you still have the originals in menyooStuff\Spooner under
their old names, nothing breaks: a placement whose model is already standing
within a third of a metre is skipped, so each thing is built once either way.
Deleting the originals is tidier and costs nothing.

A shipped file can also name the saves it was made from, in its Note:

  <Note>absorbs: newnew, newlamar</Note>

and those files are skipped in menyooStuff\Spooner from then on, so a thing
taken out of the shipped copy -- a sign, a model swapped for one with collision
-- does not come back from the original. Eight of the files here name theirs.

TAKING SOMETHING OUT
--------------------
The spooner records what you PUT somewhere. There is no tag anywhere in its
format for what you took AWAY -- so a cupboard you deleted to make room for the
scale is back next session, standing through the middle of everything you
arranged around it.

So a scene file may also carry a Removals block, which Menyoo ignores when it
loads one:

  <Removals>
    <Remove>
      <HashName>prop_bin_07a</HashName>
      <ModelHash>0x0d33bb1d</ModelHash>
      <X>-14.2</X><Y>-1438.6</Y><Z>31.1</Z>
      <Radius>1.5</Radius>
    </Remove>
  </Removals>

Every copy of that model inside that sphere is hidden while the scene is up, and
put back when you are well past it. The radius is small on purpose: hiding a bin
at Denise's must not hide the one outside Gerald's.

You do not have to write it by hand. Open the mod's settings, find "Spooner
scenes", look straight at the thing you want gone and press "Hide the prop I am
looking at". It goes immediately and is written into hidden.xml in this folder,
so it is gone from then on as well. It will not take one of its own scene's
props, and it will not take a person or a car.

WHAT IS KEPT
------------
Position and rotation, whether it is frozen, visible and invincible, and for a
ped: the weapon, the outfit, the relationship group, the health and armour,
and whatever it was doing -- a scenario or an animation.

A ped saved with NOTHING to do is not left standing there empty-handed. He is
given an idle that suits what he is holding: a man with a weapon stands guard,
a man without one hangs out, smokes, drinks or is on his phone -- and the same
man gets the same idle every session, because it is picked from where he
stands rather than at random.

Scenery peds live a little. Every minute or four one of them changes what he
is doing, stretches his legs -- walks a few metres off, stands about, walks
back -- or walks off altogether and is back on his mark some minutes later,
walking in from wherever he went. Nobody is ever cut in or out where you can
see it. And in the small hours -- three to seven on the game's clock unless
you change it -- nobody is there at all: they walk off as the hour strikes and
drift back after it. A man the spooner gave a scenario or an animation keeps
it, comes back to it, and takes his walks half as often. All of it is one
setting away: PedsLive=false leaves everybody stood on his mark.

They still do not startle, flee or pick fights of their own, because a scene
that has emptied itself five minutes after you first see it is not scenery.
The set's own join a war on the block and come back to their marks after it.

A file can name the ones who never leave, in its Note, by their saved handle:

  <Note>stays: 463969, 504650</Note>

for the man behind the counter and the two on the pipe at the camp.

A FLOOR UNDER A SLAB THAT HAS NONE
----------------------------------
Some models are pictures: the destruction pieces (des_...) that the game shows
while a building comes down carry no collision of their own, so a slab of them
stood up on open ground is one you fall straight through. The log says so:
"had no body; the model has no collision to give it". Name the placement in
the file's Note, by its saved handle, and a floor is laid under it -- invisible
building blocks, measured against the slab, their tops exactly at its top:

  <Note>floors: 204034@30.85</Note>

The number after @ is where the top of it is, as a height in the world -- the
Z you read off the screen stood on it. Leave it off and the mod uses the height
the scene's other props stand at inside the slab, and failing that the model's
bounding box, which can be metres above the ground you see (Parkview's was).
Only named ones get a floor, because a destruction model can as easily be a
wall as a floor. The log line names the handle to write. Parkview had two
named until its ground pieces came out; the clause is what to write when the
next slab goes in.

SETTINGS  (Hoodrich.ini, [Scenery])
-----------------------------------
Enabled     = true    Build saved scenes at all.
FromMenyoo  = false   Read menyooStuff\Spooner as well as this folder.
Range       = 220     How near you have to be, in metres.
PedsLive    = true    The people come and go. See above.
QuietFrom   = 3       The hours, on the game's clock, that nobody is about.
QuietTo     = 7       The same number in both means never.

IF YOU HAVE NOT SAVED YET
-------------------------
Whatever you placed this session is still only in memory. Open the mod's
settings, find "Spooner scenes", and press "Save everything around me" -- it
writes the placed peds, cars and props within 80 m into this folder as a scene
file and lists every one of them in Hoodrich.log. The file opens in Menyoo too.
