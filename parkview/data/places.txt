PARKVIEW PLACES
===============
A marker on the ground with a blip on the map, that takes you somewhere. One
per line, four fields separated by | :

    X,Y,Z,heading | X,Y,Z,heading | blip | name
    <-- the marker -->  <-- where it puts you -->

The first group is where you stand to be offered it. The second is where it
puts you. Blip is the sprite number -- 680 is the casino table-games die, 40
is the house. Everything after the third bar is the name, and it is what the
prompt, the feed and the map call it.

A line starting with # is ignored, and so is anything without three bars.

SETTING THE INSIDE WITHOUT EDITING THIS FILE: walk in by the marker, stand
where you want to come out, and press the set-room key (Alt+F9). It is written
to places.sav next to the log and this file is left alone. That is the way to
try rooms until one is right.

THE MOTEL IS REAL -- walked into on 2026-09-22, bed and lamp and Vespucci Blvd
on the minimap -- so the destination below is confirmed, not a guess. What it
is NOT is its own room: the dice game currently comes out at the far end of the
same motel the apartment door uses. Walk in by the die, find somewhere that
suits, press Alt+F9, and it gets one of its own.
