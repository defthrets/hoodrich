PARKVIEW
========

A scene on the open ground in Chamberlain Hills: three apartment blocks, houses, a shop
front, a camp, cars, fences, lights, rubbish -- and eighty-odd people who live there.
They idle with the thing in their hand, talk to each other out loud, throw signs, stretch
their legs, walk off and walk back in, and are not about in the small hours. The hobos go
through the bins and the wrecks. Nobody is ever cut in or out where you can see it.

Built by spitmux, with the same loader Posted Up uses for its scenes. Runs on its own:
one dll, one ini, one folder of scene files. No other mod needed.

POSTED UP HAS PARKVIEW BUILT IN (from 2026-09-25). If you run Posted Up, you do not need
Parkview.dll: Posted Up's Hoodrich.dll carries this whole mod and uses the same
scripts\Parkview.ini and scripts\Parkview\ folder, so your scenes, captures and room are
exactly where they were. Keep one or the other -- if both are in scripts, the second to
start says so in the log and stands down rather than building everything twice.

INSTALL
-------
1. ScriptHookV (dev-c.com) and ScriptHookVDotNet 3 -- 3.6 or newer.
2. Unblock the zip (right-click, Properties, Unblock) before extracting.
3. Drag the scripts folder from the zip into your GTA V folder. Legacy or Enhanced.

You get:
  scripts\Parkview.dll
  scripts\Parkview.ini
  scripts\Parkview\scenery\parkview-apartments.xml   the scene
  scripts\Parkview\scenery\READ ME.txt                how scenes work

The log is scripts\Parkview\Parkview.log.

WHERE
-----
Chamberlain Hills, the open ground west of the court off Davis Avenue. Around
X -277, Y -1640. It builds as you come within 220 m.

KEYS
----
F9         save everything placed within 80 m of you to a scene file
Shift+F9   read the scene files again and rebuild
Ctrl+F9    hide the map prop you are looking at, for good

All three are in Parkview.ini.

MAKING YOUR OWN SCENES
----------------------
Arrange anything in Menyoo's Object Spooner, save it, and either set FromMenyoo=true in
the ini or copy the file into scripts\Parkview\scenery. scenery\READ ME.txt has the
details: which peds stay, which get a floor laid under them, what a particular man does.

RUNNING WITH POSTED UP
----------------------
Fine. Posted Up no longer ships Parkview, so nothing doubles up.
