POSTED UP 0.4.0
===============

The city has an opinion now.

Until this release the map was scenery. One corner was as good as any other, so
the correct play was to find a decent one and never leave it; the stash house
could not be touched, so the correct play was to put everything in it; and two
dealers ran an economy that had nine gangs and ninetyseven neighbourhoods behind
it. All three of those are different now.

Also, please read the first line of the FIXES section. It could have cost you a
save and it has been in every build until this one.


NEW -- WHERE YOU STAND MATTERS
------------------------------

Every gram you sell on a block makes the next customer there slower to turn up,
and it recovers while you are somewhere else. It moves how OFTEN somebody walks
up and never what they pay, so an eighth is still an eighth's money -- a
worked-out corner goes quiet, it does not start haggling.

An ounce barely registers. A kilo dries a block for an hour. The wheel says how
worked over the block is under "This block", and the price readout says it too,
because a slowdown you caused yourself and cannot see is indistinguishable from
a bug.

And it works both ways. Word gets round on the feed that somewhere is busy, and
it is -- the block a tip names genuinely pays better until it wears off. That is
the first time anything on that feed has been worth acting on rather than worth
having caused. It never names the block you are already standing on.

  [Economy]  BlockSaturationEnabled, BlockSaturationGrams, BlockRecoveryMinutes,
             BlockDemandFloor
  [Socials]  BlockTipsEnabled, BlockTipEveryMinutes, BlockTipChancePercent,
             BlockTipMinutes, BlockTipBoost


NEW -- THE HOUSE CAN BE TAKEN FROM YOU
--------------------------------------

Nothing in the game had any claim on what was in Denise's, which made three
hundred kilos of free storage into a bank and quietly made the dead drop
pointless. Get loud enough, hold enough, and somebody comes while you are out --
police if it is heat that brought them, whoever you are beefing with if it is
not. They take a share, not the lot.

You are texted first, minutes before anybody turns up. Move it to the drop,
carry it, sell it off, or take the hit -- all four are legitimate. Being at the
house when they arrive calls it off outright, and not as a dice roll: the message
said come home, and you came home.

Under 250g nobody is interested, so the threat arrives with the hoard rather than
landing on somebody who has nothing to lose yet. A quiet dealer with a full house
is safe, which is the lesson the dead drop was built to teach and never got to.

  [Hideouts]  StashRaidsEnabled, StashRaidChancePercent, StashRaidTakePercent,
              StashRaidWarningMinutes


NEW -- TWELVE MORE PLUGS
------------------------

Supply had Gerald and the port. Four of the seven products -- crack, meth, coke
and heroin -- had no street source at all and could only be bought by the kilo
off a container.

Nine gang corners, one per set, selling only what that set moves, all at three
quarters of the street price and all stepped on. Three independents where no set
holds: meth out past the yards after dark, coke on the west side at night, crack
and brown in La Mesa around the clock.

That is a real choice rather than a better option. The corner is cheap because
what he hands you has already been halved -- fine, until you want to cut it
yourself and find there is nothing left to cut. The independent is dear and
clean.

Some nights the same man on the same corner has something nobody has touched,
and he wants paying for it. The cheap option is briefly the expensive one, worth
it, and gone tomorrow.


NEW -- PLUGS RING YOU
---------------------

An offer lands in Messages: one man, one product, a real discount, and a clock.
His price drops while it stands and nobody else's does, and the wheel shows what
is left on the clock when you get to him.

This is what the Messages app was missing. Until now it only ever held things
that had already finished happening.

  [Supply]  ColdCallsEnabled, ColdCallEveryMinutes, ColdCallChancePercent,
            ColdCallMinutes


FIXES
-----

* A SAVE THAT COULD NOT BE READ WAS TREATED AS NO SAVE AT ALL. A locked file, an
  antivirus scanner or one truncated write, and the mod logged "starting fresh at
  rank 0" and then autosaved a blank state over the top of your playthrough. Two
  saves after that the rolling backup had rolled too and it was gone.

  Worse, the backup looked like insurance. One was written on every save since
  the feature existed and nothing had ever opened it.

  The mod now knows the difference between missing and unreadable. Missing still
  starts a new game. Unreadable loads the backup, and if that will not open
  either, saving is switched off for the session and it tells you on screen
  rather than in a log.

* Backing out of any screen threw a punch. The previous release added three melee
  controls and none of them was the one doing it -- backspace and pad B land on
  MELEE_ATTACK_LIGHT, which no screen was disabling. The list had been typed out
  by hand eleven times and nine of the copies were missing it. There is one list
  now.

* Tao teleported into his car at the end of a delivery. A timeout on the enter
  task does not mean "give up", it means "be in that seat by then", and the game
  keeps that promise by warping. He walks to the door and opens it now, and so do
  rollers and homies.

* Tao introduced himself on repeat port runs to a man he had personally handed a
  brick to. His greeting was keyed off a number that only moves when Gerald pays,
  so a run that reached the yard and died on the way home reset him.

* Repeat missions paid first-run rates. They pay a fraction now, per mission.

* The tag list ran dry for good after five runs. The rival goes back over
  whatever has been up longest, which is what Lamar has been saying about him all
  along.

* HideoutStashCapacity was 5000 in the code and 300000 in the ini, so it depended
  on whether you happened to have the file. Both say 300000.

* "Spin Jamestown" is called "Spin Rancho", which is where it has always been.

* Gerald's fence. Seven trucks in, he has done the math.

* 317KB of icons that nothing referenced are gone.


UPGRADING
---------

Drop it over the top. Old saves load and nothing needs restarting -- port runs
you have already paid for are counted as yard visits so Tao remembers you.

Hoodrich.ini is not overwritten. Every new setting has a working default, so the
release runs correctly without touching it; copy the new blocks across if you
want to tune them.

If you want none of the new pressure: BlockSaturationEnabled=false,
StashRaidsEnabled=false, ColdCallsEnabled=false, BlockTipsEnabled=false. That is
0.3.0 behaviour with the fixes.
